# Workshop promised-times policy

**Policy owner:** Service Manager (each site) · **Applies to:** every job card in a Harbourline workshop · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

To give every customer a promised time we keep, to tell them early when we cannot, and to make sure no work is done that the customer has not agreed to.

## Promise windows

1. A scheduled service booked for the morning is promised by **3:00 pm** the same day; booked for the afternoon, by **10:00 am** the next business day.
2. Diagnosis and repair cards carry a promised time set by the advisor after the technician's first look, never at booking alone.
3. The promised time is recorded on the job card at booking and confirmed at **Check in**. A card whose promised time passes without **Complete** is **overdue** and shows on the board.
4. A promised time may be changed only by the advisor, with an advisor_note memo stating the new time and the reason, and only after the customer has been told.

## Waiting customers

- A customer who waits on site is marked **Waiting** at check-in. Their card goes to the top of the technician's queue.
- Waiting work is limited to jobs under two hours. Longer work is rebooked or offered a loan car.
- Update a waiting customer at least every 45 minutes, in person.

## Loan cars

- Loan cars are offered for work promised beyond the same day, for warranty and campaign work over two hours, and to customers without transport.
- Record the loan car on the job card at check-in, with the customer's licence sighted (last four digits and expiry only). A loan car must be returned and checked before the card can move to **Collected**.
- Fuel is returned at the level received; tolls and fines during the loan are the customer's.

## Communicating a delay

1. As soon as a delay is known, the advisor calls the customer (not a text first) and records a call memo with the outcome.
2. Offer the earliest new promised time, a loan car if the delay passes the day, and an explanation that names the cause (part on order, extra work found, technician availability).
3. A delay caused by a part on order needs a parts_note memo with the supplier and expected date.

## Approval of extra work

Work found that was not on the customer's requests needs approval before it is done. Thresholds are per card, customer-pay, GST inclusive:

| Amount | Who may approve | How it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Up to $350 | The technician, if the work is needed to complete the requested job safely | tech_note memo naming the work and the amount |
| $350 to $1,500 | The service advisor, after telling the customer by phone | advisor_note memo with the customer's verbal approval and the time |
| Over $1,500 | The customer, in writing (email or SMS reply, or signature on the quote) | the written approval attached as a document; the card moves to awaiting_approval until it arrives |

Warranty and campaign work needs no customer approval but must be recorded as such on the line.

## Rules

- No extra work without the memo and approval above. Lines added without approval are marked **needs approval** and are not invoiced.
- Keep the promised time honest: it is better to promise later and deliver early.
- The job card is the record. If it is not on the card, it did not happen.
