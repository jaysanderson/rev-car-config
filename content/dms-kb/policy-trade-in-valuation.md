# Trade-in valuation policy

**Policy owner:** Used Vehicle Manager · **Applies to:** every trade-in taken on a new-vehicle deal · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

To value trade-ins consistently and honestly, keep a record that supports the price, and stop over-allowances being used as hidden discounts.

## Who may appraise

Only staff who have completed the Harbourline appraisal course may sign a trade-in appraisal form. Salespeople may collect details and photographs, but the valuation is set by the used vehicle manager or a trained appraiser at the site.

## The appraisal

Every trade-in needs a completed **trade-in appraisal form** uploaded to the deal as a document of kind **trade_in_form**. The form records:

1. **Owner** – registered owner's name, matched against the registration certificate. If the customer is not the registered owner, written authority from the owner is required.
2. **Registration** – plate and state, and registration expiry.
3. **VIN** – read from the vehicle's compliance plate or windscreen, not from paperwork alone.
4. **Make, model, year and variant.**
5. **Odometer** in kilometres, read from the instrument cluster and photographed.
6. **Condition** – body, paint, glass, interior, tyres (tread depth in mm), mechanical, warning lights, service history, keys (number), and any accident or hail damage.
7. **Finance owing** – checked on the Personal Property Securities Register (PPSR). The PPSR certificate is attached.
8. **Photographs** – four corners, interior, odometer and any damage.

## Setting the valuation

- The **valuation** is the price Harbourline expects to achieve wholesale, less reconditioning cost. It is based on the current market guide, recent auction results for comparable vehicles and the reconditioning estimate.
- Reconditioning estimate must be itemised (tyres, paint, interior, service, roadworthy items).
- Vehicles with unrepaired structural damage, flood damage, a written-off status or odometer tampering are valued for wholesale disposal only.
- The valuation is recorded on the trade-in record in the DMS. It is not shown to the customer as a separate number unless they ask.

## The offer

- The **offered** amount is what the customer is told they will receive. It normally equals the valuation.
- **Over-allowance** (offered above valuation) is a concession under the discount approval matrix. It must be approved by the sales manager or general manager as the matrix requires.
- **Under-allowance** (offered below valuation) is permitted only to cover a specific defect found after the appraisal and must be explained to the customer in writing.
- Never quote a trade-in figure that combines the trade value with a discount on the new car. Each must be shown separately on the quote and contract.

## Validity

A trade-in offer is honoured for **7 days** if the vehicle's condition and kilometres do not change materially (more than 500 km or new damage). After 7 days, or if the condition changes, the vehicle must be re-appraised.

## Payout and PPSR

- If finance is owing, Harbourline pays the lender directly at settlement and the payout amount is deducted from the trade-in value on the contract.
- If the payout is more than the offer (negative equity), the difference must be shown on the contract. It may only be added to a new finance amount with the lender's knowledge and the customer's written acknowledgement.

## At delivery

1. Re-check odometer and condition. If they have changed materially, stop and re-appraise.
2. Collect all keys, the service book, owner's manual and the registration papers.
3. The customer signs the transfer section and the trade-in declaration (no undisclosed damage, no finance other than declared).
4. Record the trade-in's arrival into used stock in the DMS.

## Using AI extraction

When the appraisal form is processed with **Process with ARAG**, the registration, VIN, make, model, year, km, condition notes and owner are extracted. The VIN and km must always be verified against the form before clicking **Verify**, because they drive the PPSR check and the value.

## Records

Appraisal forms, photographs and PPSR certificates are kept with the deal for seven years.
