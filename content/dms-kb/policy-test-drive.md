# Test-drive policy

**Policy owner:** Sales Manager (each site) · **Applies to:** every test drive in a Harbourline demonstrator or stock vehicle · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

To keep customers, staff and vehicles safe, protect the group from uninsured loss, and give every customer a drive that answers their questions about the car.

## Licence requirements

1. The driver must hold a **current, full or provisional (P1 or P2) driver licence** valid for the vehicle class. Overseas licences are accepted if they are in English or have an approved translation, and the holder is a visitor eligible to drive in NSW.
2. **Sight the physical or digital licence** before the drive and record in the DMS the licence's last four digits and expiry date (**Check licence**). Do not photocopy or upload a licence for a standard drive.
3. **Learner drivers** may drive only when accompanied by a fully licensed supervising driver in the front seat, and only vehicles learners may drive. A salesperson may act as supervisor only if they hold a full licence and agree.
4. **P-platers** may not drive vehicles prohibited for provisional drivers in NSW. Check before booking performance models (for example GR Yaris, GR Corolla, GR86) and do not book a prohibited vehicle for a P-plater.
5. Anyone who appears affected by alcohol or drugs, or who cannot produce a licence, may not drive. Offer a passenger drive instead.

## Insurance and excess

- Demonstrators and stock vehicles are insured by Harbourline for test drives by licensed drivers who meet this policy.
- The customer must sign the **test-drive agreement**, which states that the customer is responsible for the **insurance excess of $2,500** if damage is caused by their driving (higher, at $5,000, for drivers under 25 and for GR models), and for any traffic or toll fines incurred during the drive.
- Explain the excess before the customer signs. Do not suggest it will not be enforced.
- Report any incident to the sales manager immediately and record it on the test drive.

## Accompanied drives

- A standard drive is **about 30 minutes**, accompanied by a salesperson.
- The salesperson sits in the front passenger seat, explains the controls before setting off, and does not distract the driver.
- Maximum of four occupants including the salesperson.
- Children may ride only in an approved restraint appropriate to their age.

## Routes

Each site has an approved route of about 15–20 km that includes suburban streets, a roundabout, a parking manoeuvre and a motorway or arterial section:

| Site | Route |
|---|---|
| Parramatta | Church St, James Ruse Dr, M4 to Silverwater Rd and back via Parramatta Rd |
| Hornsby | Pacific Hwy, M1 to Berowra and back via the Old Pacific Hwy |
| Penrith | Mulgoa Rd, M4 to the Northern Rd and back; towing route by appointment |
| Liverpool | Hume Hwy, M5 to Moorebank Ave and back |

Use the approved route unless the customer has a specific need (for example their own driveway or a steep street). Avoid school zones during school-zone hours, and never demonstrate hard acceleration or performance driving on public roads.

## Extended and home drives

- **Extended drives** of up to 24 hours on selected demonstrators require sales-manager approval, a licence copy uploaded as a document of kind **licence**, the signed extended-drive agreement, and a check that the customer has a genuine intention to buy.
- **Home or workplace drives** within 15 km of a site are available on weekdays, accompanied.
- **Towing drives** are run from Penrith with the demonstrator caravan, by appointment, only with a vehicle rated for the caravan's mass.

## Incentives

If the customer was offered a test-drive incentive at booking (shown on the test drive and the handover sheet), honour exactly that incentive. Do not substitute or add incentives.

## After the drive

1. Walk around the car with the customer and note any damage.
2. Record the outcome in the DMS (**Complete**) the same day.
3. No-shows: click **No show** and call the customer the same day.

## Records

Signed test-drive agreements are kept for two years. Licence copies for extended drives are deleted 90 days after the drive unless a deal is created.
