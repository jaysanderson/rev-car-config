# Delivery checklist and PDI

**Policy owner:** Sales Manager (each site) with Service Manager · **Applies to:** every new-vehicle delivery · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

A delivery is the last thing the customer remembers about buying from us and the first thing they remember about owning the car. This policy makes every handover complete, safe and on time.

## Before a deal can move to delivery

The deal must be at stage **contract** with:

1. Required approvals recorded (discount approval matrix).
2. A signed contract uploaded and **verified**, with no Mismatch badge.
3. Deposit received.
4. Finance **approved** with conditions met, or proof of funds for cash buyers.
5. A **delivery date** agreed with the customer.

## Pre-delivery inspection (PDI)

Book the PDI with the service department at least **3 business days** before delivery (2 days for ready-now units already on lot). The workshop completes the Toyota PDI checklist and records it against the VIN:

- Remove transport protection; wash, vacuum and detail.
- Fluid levels; tyre pressures to placard; battery state of charge (12 V and, for hybrids and EVs, high-voltage system health check).
- All lights, wipers, horn, windows, locks, keys (both keys present and programmed).
- Infotainment set up; software updates applied; connected services activated or ready to activate.
- Safety systems: warning lights clear, camera and sensor operation.
- Fitted accessories installed and checked (tow bar, roof racks, mats, tint, protection film) against the deal's accessory list and supplier invoice.
- Road test (minimum 5 km).
- Paint and body inspection under lights; any defect fixed before delivery, never "after".

PDI sign-off is required before registration is lodged.

## Registration and settlement

- Lodge registration in the customer's name exactly as on the contract.
- For financed deals, settlement must be confirmed by the lender before the car leaves, unless the business manager confirms an approved settlement arrangement.
- Trade-in payout arranged with the trade-in lender.

## Delivery day checklist

The salesperson completes these in the DMS on the deal's **Delivery** card:

1. **Vehicle ready** – PDI signed off, washed, fuel at least a quarter tank (EV charged to at least 80%).
2. **Paperwork** – registration papers, contract copy, finance documents, insurance certificate of currency (from the customer's insurer if not arranged by us), service and warranty booklet.
3. **Identity** – customer's licence sighted; name matches contract.
4. **Trade-in** – re-check odometer and condition, collect keys and papers (see trade-in valuation policy).
5. **Walk-around** – features, safety systems, hybrid or EV charging, phone pairing, connected-services app, first service interval and how to book.
6. **Accessories** – show each accessory fitted.
7. **Promotions and bonuses** – confirm each line on the contract has been delivered (for example servicing included, accessory bundle fitted).
8. **Customer signs** the delivery receipt confirming condition, odometer and items received.
9. **Odometer at handover** recorded.
10. Click **Mark delivered**. The deal moves to **delivered**, the unit to **sold**, and the lead to **won**.

## Timing

- Deliveries are booked in 60-minute slots (90 minutes for hybrids, EVs and GR models).
- Delivering a car before the PDI is signed off, or before settlement is confirmed, is not permitted.
- If a delivery date will be missed, the customer must be told at least **24 hours** before, with a new date.

## After delivery

- **Day 2:** salesperson calls to check the customer is happy and answer questions.
- **Day 7:** the customer receives the satisfaction survey.
- **Day 30:** reminder about the first service and connected-services set-up.

## Problems found at delivery

If the customer finds a defect, do not argue. Record it on the delivery receipt, agree a fix date, and tell the sales manager. The customer may choose to delay delivery.
