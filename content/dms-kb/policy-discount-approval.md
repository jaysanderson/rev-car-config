# Discount approval matrix

**Policy owner:** General Manager · **Applies to:** all new-vehicle retail deals at Parramatta, Hornsby, Penrith and Liverpool · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

To give salespeople room to close deals quickly while protecting the group's gross profit, and to make sure every concession has a named approver recorded on the deal.

## Definitions

- **Discount** – any reduction from the vehicle list price entered in the deal's Discount field.
- **Total concession** – discount + ready-now bonus + the dealer-funded cost of any promotion + any **trade-in over-allowance** (the amount offered above the appraised valuation). Approvals are based on total concession, not just the discount field.
- **Front gross** – vehicle price + accessories − landed cost − total concession. Calculated by the DMS on the deal.
- **Floor gross** – $800 front gross per new vehicle. Deals below the floor need general-manager approval.
- **Bonus headroom** – the ready-now bonus amount Domo publishes for a specific aged unit.

## The matrix

| Approver | Total concession | Front gross after concession | Ready-now bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salesperson | Up to $500 | $1,500 or more | Not permitted |
| Sales manager (own site) | Up to $2,000 | $800 or more | Up to the unit's bonus headroom |
| General manager | Above $2,000 | Below $800, including negative | Above the unit's bonus headroom |

**Both conditions in a row must be met.** If either the amount or the gross falls outside a row, the next approver up is required. For example:

- $400 concession, front gross $1,900 → salesperson can approve.
- $400 concession, front gross $1,200 → sales manager (gross below $1,500).
- $1,800 concession, front gross $950 → sales manager.
- $2,500 concession, front gross $900 → general manager (amount above $2,000).
- $1,200 concession, front gross $650 → general manager (below floor).
- RAV4 HL-2231 with a $1,800 ready-now bonus and headroom $1,800 → sales manager. A $2,100 bonus on the same unit → general manager.

The sales manager at another site cannot approve for your site. When the sales manager is absent, the general manager approves.

## Back gross

Finance and insurance commission (back gross) may not be used to justify a front-gross concession below the floor. A deal is approved on front gross. Customers must never be told that a discount depends on taking finance or insurance through the dealer; see the finance and insurance compliance policy.

## How approval is recorded

1. Enter the discount, bonus and promotions on the deal. The DMS shows **Approval needed: Salesperson / Sales manager / General manager** next to the Pricing card.
2. Move the deal to **approval** and click **Request approval**.
3. The approver opens the deal, reviews pricing, gross and the AI summary's risks, and clicks **Approve deal** or **Decline** with a note.
4. The approval is recorded as a deal event with the approver's name, time and the concession approved.
5. If pricing changes after approval, the approval is cancelled and the deal returns to **approval**.

A contract must not be issued on a deal without the required approval recorded. The DMS blocks **Move to contract** until it is.

## Fleet, government and staff sales

Fleet, government and staff deals are priced from separate schedules and are approved by the general manager regardless of amount.

## Promotions

A published promotion's dealer-funded cost counts towards total concession, but the promotion itself was approved when it was published. If the only concession on a deal is a published promotion within its rules, no further approval is needed.

## Trade-in over-allowance

Offering more for a trade-in than its appraised valuation is a concession. It counts towards total concession and must be approved the same way. See the trade-in valuation policy.

## Breaches

Issuing a contract above your approval limit, splitting a concession across fields to stay under a limit, or promising a discount verbally before approval is a breach of this policy and will be reviewed by the general manager.

## Quick reference

- Up to $500 and gross at least $1,500: you.
- Up to $2,000 and gross at least $800, or a ready-now bonus within headroom: your sales manager.
- Anything else: the general manager.
