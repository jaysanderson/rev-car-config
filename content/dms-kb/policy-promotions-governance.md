# Promotions governance

**Policy owner:** General Manager · **Applies to:** every promotion shown in the configurator, on a quote, in a campaign or in a follow-up message · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

Promotions move stock and win customers, but a wrong or misleading promotion costs gross and trust and can breach consumer law. This policy sets out who decides what, so that every promotion is based on data, worded accurately, and approved by an accountable person.

## The three-step model

| Step | Who | What they do | What they may not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Select | **Domo** (`dealer_signals`, `aged_stock_actions`) | Identifies target models and units (aged stock, long days of supply, target gap) and the incentive headroom for each | Decide wording or publish |
| 2. Compose | **Progress Agentic RAG** | Drafts the title, customer wording, kind, value, eligibility and internal reason, grounded in dealer policy and product content | Exceed headroom; promise anything not in policy; publish |
| 3. Publish | **Sales manager or general manager** | Reviews the draft and clicks **Publish**, or edits, or rejects | Publish a promotion they have not read in full |

The digital experience manager may request and edit drafts and attach published promotions to campaigns, but cannot publish.

## Rules for every promotion

1. **Value within headroom.** The dealer-funded value per unit must not exceed the Domo headroom for the eligible units. Promotions above headroom need general-manager approval.
2. **Clear eligibility.** State the models, grades or specific stock units, whether aged stock only, and the end date. Every eligible unit must actually qualify.
3. **End date required.** No open-ended promotions. Maximum 90 days.
4. **One line on the quote.** Each promotion appears as its own line on quotes and contracts.
5. **Provenance kept.** The Domo rows, the knowledge-base passages cited when composing, and the publisher's name and time are stored with the promotion.
6. **No conditional finance tie.** A promotion must not require the customer to take dealer finance or insurance unless it is a lender-funded finance offer with all conditions disclosed.

## The honesty rule

Promotions must be true for every customer who sees them.

- **Say why.** Ready-now bonuses on aged stock say that the car has been in stock longer. Do not call aged stock "new arrival", "just landed" or similar.
- **No false scarcity or urgency.** "Last one", "only today" or countdowns are allowed only if literally true across all four sites.
- **No invented value.** A "bonus worth $1,500" must cost Harbourline about that much at retail. Do not inflate accessory or servicing values.
- **No hidden conditions.** Conditions that would change a customer's decision go in the main wording, not only in fine print.
- **Drive-away price is the price.** Advertised drive-away prices include all on-road costs and the promotion.
- **AI drafts are drafts.** Follow-up messages, handover sheets and configurator offers may mention only **published** promotions that apply to the customer's car. If none applies, they say nothing about promotions. Staff must never send a message that invents an offer.

## Reviewing a draft (checklist for publishers)

1. Open the draft on **Promotions → Composed** and click **Provenance**.
2. Check the Domo refresh time is from the last 24 hours.
3. Check value against headroom for each eligible unit.
4. Read the customer wording against the honesty rule.
5. Check the end date and eligibility.
6. Click **Publish** or **Edit**. Rejected drafts are deleted with a note.

## Withdrawing a promotion

Click **Withdraw** when stock sells, Domo no longer flags the target, or an error is found. Withdrawal removes it from the configurator and campaigns immediately. Quotes already issued with the promotion are honoured until they expire (7 days).

## Errors

If a published promotion is found to be wrong (wrong value, wrong eligibility, misleading wording), withdraw it immediately and tell the general manager. Customers who relied on it are dealt with fairly, normally by honouring the promotion as advertised.

## Records

Promotions, their provenance and publisher records are kept for 2 years after the end date.
