# Supplier terms policy

**Policy owner:** Service Manager and Dealer Principal · **Applies to:** every purchase of parts, accessories, tyres, sublet work, consumables and towing · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## Purpose

To buy from suppliers we have checked, on terms we have agreed, with every invoice matched to the job or the car it was for.

## Approved suppliers

1. Purchases are made only from suppliers whose record in the DMS is **active**. New suppliers are created on hold and approved by the service manager after an ABN check, a credit check and agreed terms.
2. A supplier is placed **on hold** after two unresolved SLA memos in a quarter, an unpaid credit, or a quality failure. The reason is recorded as a quality memo.
3. Genuine Toyota parts are bought through the Toyota parts channel. Aftermarket parts need the customer's agreement on the job card.

## Terms

- Standard terms are **30 days** from invoice date. Different terms must be on the supplier record.
- Early-payment discounts are taken when offered; the finance team records them against the invoice.

## Three-quote rule

- Any single purchase or sublet job over **$2,000** needs three written quotes attached as documents to the job card or stock unit, and the choice recorded as an advisor_note memo. Toyota genuine parts and warranty work are exempt.

## Invoice matching

1. Every supplier invoice is uploaded to the supplier record with **Upload invoice** and processed with Progress Agentic RAG.
2. The invoice is matched to a job card (customer-pay or warranty work) or a stock unit (accessories fitted, reconditioning) within five business days. Unmatched invoices appear under Open invoices on the supplier.
3. A line on the invoice that differs from the quote or the menu price is recorded as a parts memo on the job card, and the invoice is paid at its price unless disputed.

## Disputes and credits

- Record a dispute as an sla memo (late, wrong part, damaged) or a credit memo (credit requested) with the invoice number before calling the supplier, so the record is complete.
- A credit received is recorded as a credit memo with the credit note number and attached as a document.

## Rules

- No purchase from an on-hold supplier without the service manager's approval recorded as a memo.
- No invoice is paid without a match.
- Supplier contacts' personal details stay on the supplier record, not in job card memos.
