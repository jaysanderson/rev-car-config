# Harbourline Toyota policies

Harbourline Toyota operates four sites in Western and Northern Sydney. Our home site is **Parramatta**; we also operate **Hornsby**, **Penrith** and **Liverpool**. Stock is shared across all four sites, so a car shown at any site can be delivered at Parramatta or at the site closest to you.

## Ready-now bonus (aged stock)

Some vehicles have been in our stock longer than others, for example because a colour or grade combination was less popular, or because a newer model year has arrived. We think customers deserve to know this, so we explain it openly.

- Vehicles that have been in stock for **60 days or more** may carry a **ready-now bonus**: a price reduction, free accessory pack, or bonus on your trade-in.
- The bonus is larger the longer the car has been in stock, and is always shown on the quote as a separate line item.
- An aged-stock vehicle is brand new and unregistered; it carries the full new-vehicle warranty from the date you take delivery. The build date may be earlier than a factory-ordered car, which can matter slightly at resale. We will tell you the build month if you ask.
- Ready-now vehicles can be delivered in as little as 2-3 business days.

## Transfers between our sites

| Site | Distance from Parramatta | Transfer run |
|---|---|---|
| Parramatta (home) | - | Hub |
| Hornsby | about 28 km | Thursday |
| Liverpool | about 22 km | Thursday |
| Penrith | about 34 km | Friday |

A vehicle at another site can be moved on that site's scheduled run at no cost. A car at Hornsby or Liverpool requested by Tuesday afternoon will usually be in Parramatta on Thursday; a car at Penrith requested by Wednesday will usually arrive on Friday. Urgent transfers outside the runs can be arranged, subject to driver availability.

## 48-hour holds

- You can place a **free 48-hour hold** on any ready-now vehicle. During the hold, the car is taken off sale and no other customer can reserve it.
- Holds can be placed online, by phone or in person. One hold per customer at a time.
- To keep the car after 48 hours, a fully refundable deposit (typically $1,000) converts the hold into a reservation until finance and paperwork are complete.
- If the car is being transferred between sites, the 48 hours start when it arrives.

## Test drives

- Test drives are free and can be booked online or by phone at any of our four sites. Walk-ins are welcome when a demonstrator is free.
- Bring a full, current driver licence (P-platers are welcome; learners must be accompanied by a fully licensed driver and may only drive vehicles eligible under learner rules).
- A standard test drive is about 30 minutes on a route that includes suburban streets and a motorway section.
- **Extended test drives** of up to 24 hours are available on selected demonstrators for serious buyers, subject to a licence check.
- **Towing test drives** can be arranged at Penrith with a demonstrator caravan by appointment.
- We can bring a demonstrator to your home or workplace within 15 km of any site on weekdays.

## Our promise

- Every quote shows the full drive-away price with the ready-now bonus, accessories and on-road costs itemised.
- No pressure: quotes are valid for 7 days.
- Trade-in valuations are honoured for 7 days if the car's condition and kilometres do not change.
