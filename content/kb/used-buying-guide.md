# Buying a pre-owned car at Harbourline: how we match cars to people

This guide is what the "Find what's right for me" interview and the used-car assistant use when a visitor asks which pre-owned car suits them.

## The five questions

1. **Budget** (price excluding government charges): under $30,000; $30,000 to $45,000; $45,000 to $65,000; over $65,000. Add roughly 4 to 6 per cent for NSW stamp duty and registration transfer when thinking drive-away.
2. **Main use**: family and school runs; commuting; work and towing; touring and off-road; a car to enjoy on weekends.
3. **The follow-up that depends on the use**: for families, how many seats (5 or 7); for work, how much you tow (a box trailer under 750 kg, a boat or camper around 1,500 kg, a caravan 2,500 to 3,500 kg); for commuters, which fuel you prefer (hybrid, electric, no preference); for touring, whether you need low-range 4x4 or an AWD is enough; for a weekend car, manual or automatic.
4. **What matters most**: lowest kilometres; newest car; Toyota Certified warranty; lowest price.
5. **Timing**: this week (only cars on the lot now); this month (cars arriving or in reconditioning count too); just looking.

## How the match is scored

Fit starts from budget and use, then the follow-up answer. Seven-seat needs rule out five-seat cars; a 2,500 kg caravan rules out anything rated below that (HiLux, Prado, Fortuner, LandCruiser 300 and Kluger are the realistic choices, see the towing guide). Priorities re-order the shortlist: "lowest kilometres" sorts by odometer, "newest" by model year, "certified" keeps Toyota Certified cars only, "lowest price" sorts by price. Timing removes cars that are arriving or in reconditioning when the buyer needs a car this week.

## Rules of thumb our salespeople use

- A hybrid pays back for commuters doing more than 15,000 km a year; a petrol car can be the better buy under 10,000 km a year.
- Kilometres matter less than service history: a 90,000 km car with every service logged is a safer buy than a 50,000 km car with gaps.
- Toyota Certified adds a 12-month warranty and roadside; for a car still inside its 5-year new-car warranty, the balance transfers as well.
- One-owner ex-demonstrators (low kilometres, dealer-maintained) are the closest thing to new at a used price.
- Ex-fleet cars have higher kilometres but strict service compliance; check the inspection items for interior wear.
- Read the "worth knowing" notes in the dealer comments: they list what the inspection noted but did not repair.

## Watching for a car

If nothing fits, the visitor can watch a search (for example "a hybrid RAV4 under $45,000") and we tell them when a matching car is traded in or arrives. Watch counts on a listing show how many visitors are watching that car.
