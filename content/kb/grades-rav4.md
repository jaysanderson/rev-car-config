# RAV4 grades explained

The sixth-generation RAV4 (on sale from early 2026) is hybrid-only in our configurator, with four core grades. Every grade uses the same 2.5L hybrid; the differences are equipment, styling and whether AWD is available. Prices below are Harbourline's illustrative NSW drive-away estimates for the 2WD version (Edge is AWD only). AWD adds about $3,350 to the list price, which is roughly $3,500 drive-away.

| Grade | Drive | List from (before on-roads) | Drive-away NSW est. | Step from grade below |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GX | 2WD or AWD | $45,990 | $51,190 | - |
| GXL | 2WD or AWD | $48,990 | $54,390 | about +$3,200 |
| Edge | AWD only | $55,340 | $60,990 | about +$6,600 (vs GXL 2WD) |
| Cruiser | 2WD or AWD | $56,990 | $62,790 | about +$1,800 (vs Edge) |

## GX - the sensible entry point

The GX is far better equipped than entry RAV4s used to be. You get a large touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a digital driver display, LED headlights, alloy wheels and the full Toyota Safety Sense suite including adaptive cruise, lane trace assist and blind-spot monitoring.

**Who buys it:** fleet and novated-lease buyers, families who want the space and economy without paying for extras, and people replacing an older RAV4 who mainly drive around Sydney.

## GXL - the popular middle

GXL adds comfort and convenience: dual-zone climate control, a wireless phone charger, front parking sensors and a powered tailgate, plus upgraded trim details. It is a popular middle ground for families.

**Who buys it:** families who load the boot every day (the powered tailgate matters more than people expect), and buyers who park in tight shopping-centre car parks and appreciate front sensors.

**Price step:** about $3,000 list over GX.

## Edge - the outdoors grade

Edge is AWD only. It is styled for gravel and campsite use, with rugged trim, roof rails, a panoramic view monitor and AWD drive modes for trail and snow, plus downhill assist. It is not a low-range 4x4 like a Prado, but it is the most capable RAV4 on loose surfaces.

**Who buys it:** couples and families who head to the Blue Mountains, Snowy Mountains or south coast campgrounds, and those who want AWD's 1,500 kg towing for a small camper trailer.

**Price step:** about $6,350 list over GXL 2WD, or about $3,000 over GXL AWD.

## Cruiser - the comfort flagship of the core range

Cruiser adds leather-accented, heated front seats, a panoramic sunroof, a head-up display and a nine-speaker JBL sound system. It is available as 2WD or AWD.

**Who buys it:** buyers stepping down from a larger luxury SUV who still want comfort features, and long-distance drivers who value the head-up display and premium audio.

**Price step:** about $8,000 list over GXL 2WD.

## Beyond the configurator

Toyota Australia's RAV4 range also includes an XSE hybrid (AWD) and plug-in hybrid versions (XSE PHEV and GR Sport PHEV, up to about 227 kW, with a meaningful electric-only range for daily commuting). These are not in our configurator. Ask the team for pricing and lead times if a plug-in hybrid suits your driving.

## Choosing quickly

- Mostly city driving, no towing: **GX or GXL 2WD**.
- Gravel roads, snow trips, small camper trailer: **any AWD**, with **Edge** if you like the rugged look.
- Want leather, sunroof and premium audio: **Cruiser**.
