# GR Corolla: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Performance hatch. **Powertrain:** 1.6L turbo 3-cylinder, GR-Four AWD. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** not rated.

The GR Corolla puts the GR Yaris drivetrain into a wider, five-door Corolla body. In Australia it is offered as a single, well-equipped GTS grade with a choice of manual or automatic.

## Who it suits

It suits enthusiasts who need rear doors for occasional passengers. It is less extreme than a GR Yaris and more practical, but the boot is small and the ride is firm.
