# LandCruiser 300: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Large 4x4 SUV. **Powertrain:** 3.3L twin-turbo V6 diesel, full-time 4WD. **Seats:** 7. **Braked towing:** 3,500 kg.

The LandCruiser 300 is Toyota's flagship 4x4 wagon. Its twin-turbo V6 diesel has the torque to tow a 3.5 t caravan comfortably, and it is built for remote-area reliability. The range runs from the workmanlike five-seat GX up to the luxury Sahara ZX and the off-road focused GR Sport.

## Who it suits

It suits buyers towing large caravans and boats near the 3.5 t limit, grey nomads, rural and remote owners, and anyone who wants the most capable Toyota 4x4. For lighter loads, a Prado is cheaper to buy and run.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** Five seats, vinyl floor for working use; Twin-turbo V6 diesel, 10-speed auto; Adaptive cruise and AEB; Centre diff lock and low range.

**GXL:** Seven seats; Larger touchscreen with sat-nav; Front and rear sensors; Multi-terrain select and crawl control.

**VX:** Leather-accented heated/ventilated seats; Power tailgate; Panoramic view monitor; Premium audio.

**Sahara:** Rear-seat entertainment screens; Heated second row; Head-up display; Cool box.

**Sahara ZX:** Distinct ZX styling and 20-inch alloys; Adaptive variable suspension; Premium trim finishes; Road-biased tyres.

**GR Sport:** E-KDSS stabiliser disconnect; Front and rear diff locks; GR styling and sports seats; Off-road tuned suspension.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.
- **Roof & Cargo Pack** ($1,290 fitted): Genuine roof cross bars plus a lockable roof pod or cargo barrier (model dependent).

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace, adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitor, rear cross-traffic alert; not rated by ANCAP.
