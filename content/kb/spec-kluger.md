# Kluger: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Large 7-seat SUV. **Powertrain:** 2.5L hybrid, AWD. **Seats:** 7. **Braked towing:** 2,000 kg.

Kluger is Toyota's seven-seat family SUV for buyers who want a car-like drive rather than a 4x4. In Australia it is sold only as an AWD hybrid, which roughly halves fuel use compared with the old V6 while keeping 2 t of towing capacity.

## Who it suits

It suits families with three children or regular grandparent duty, school-run and sport-carpool parents, and buyers towing a camper trailer or small caravan up to 2 t. The third row is best for children and teens on longer trips.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** Seven seats with three-zone climate; Touchscreen with wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; Adaptive cruise and lane trace; 18-inch alloys.

**GXL:** Leather-accented, heated front seats; Powered tailgate; Built-in sat-nav; Smart entry and push-button start.

**Grande:** Heated and ventilated front seats; Panoramic sunroof; Head-up display; JBL premium audio; 20-inch alloys.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.
- **Roof & Cargo Pack** ($1,290 fitted): Genuine roof cross bars plus a lockable roof pod or cargo barrier (model dependent).

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace, adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitor, rear cross-traffic alert; ANCAP 5 stars (2021).

## Buying notes

If you need more than 2 t of towing or real off-road ability, step to Prado or Fortuner. The current Kluger is a mature design with a long service history in Australia.
