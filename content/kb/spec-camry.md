# Camry: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Medium sedan. **Powertrain:** 2.5L hybrid, front-wheel drive. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 400 kg.

The current-generation Camry, launched in 2025, is sold in Australia as a hybrid only. It is a large-feeling medium sedan with a long wheelbase, a very roomy rear seat and a big boot, and its fuel use is closer to a small car than a family sedan.

## Who it suits

It suits long-distance commuters on the M4 and M2, rideshare and fleet drivers, and families who prefer a sedan to an SUV. It is not intended for towing beyond a light trailer.

## Key equipment by grade

**Ascent:** 12.3-inch touchscreen with wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; 12.3-inch digital driver display; Blind-spot monitor and rear cross-traffic alert; Wireless phone charger.

**Ascent Sport:** Adds sportier styling and 17-inch alloys; Dual-zone climate control; Built-in sat-nav; Ambient lighting.

**SL:** Leather-accented, heated and ventilated front seats; Head-up display; JBL audio; Panoramic view monitor; Powered sunroof.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace, adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitor, front centre airbag; ANCAP 5 stars (2025).

## Buying notes

Every grade has the same 170 kW hybrid, so the choice is about equipment rather than performance.
