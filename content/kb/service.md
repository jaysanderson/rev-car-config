# Servicing your Toyota

## Toyota Service Advantage

New Toyotas come with **Toyota Service Advantage** capped-price servicing for the first scheduled services (generally the first five years, with a kilometre limit that depends on the model's interval). The capped price is the most you pay for each standard logbook service under normal operating conditions, and includes labour, fluids and Toyota Genuine Parts. Extra work (tyres, brake pads, wiper blades, repairs, or "severe use" items) is quoted separately.

## Intervals by type of vehicle

| Vehicle type | Typical interval | Capped-price period |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol hybrids (Yaris Cross, Corolla, Corolla Cross, C-HR, Camry, RAV4, Kluger) | 12 months or 15,000 km | First 5 years / 75,000 km |
| Diesel 4x4s and utes (Prado, LandCruiser 300, Fortuner, HiLux) | 6 months or 10,000 km | First 5 years / 100,000 km (10 services) |
| bZ4X (electric) | 12 months or 15,000 km | First 5 years |
| GR performance models | 12 months or 15,000 km (some GR models have shorter intervals; check your logbook) | First 5 years |
