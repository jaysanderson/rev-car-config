# Towing guide for the Toyota range

This guide helps you match a trailer, camper, caravan or boat to the right Toyota. Figures are the manufacturer's maximum braked towing capacities for the current (2026) range. Always check the compliance plate and owner's manual for your exact variant, because cab style, drivetrain and transmission can change the rating.

## Tow ball download

Tow ball download (the weight the trailer coupling puts on the tow ball) should normally be about 10% of the loaded trailer weight. Most Toyota 4x4s allow up to 350 kg at 3,500 kg; Kluger allows about 200 kg at its 2,000 kg limit. Too little download makes a van sway; too much overloads the rear axle and lightens the steering. Use a tow ball scale at the start of every trip.

## GCM, GVM and payload - why 3,500 kg is not the whole story

- **GVM (gross vehicle mass):** the most the vehicle itself can weigh, including passengers, luggage, accessories and the tow ball download.
- **GCM (gross combination mass):** the most the vehicle and trailer can weigh together.
- In many 4x4s and utes, GCM is less than GVM plus the 3,500 kg towing rating. That means if you tow the full 3,500 kg you may have to carry much less in the vehicle than its normal payload. A HiLux or Prado loaded with family, bar work, a fridge and water may only be able to tow 2,800-3,000 kg legally.

## What you need for common loads

| Load | Typical loaded weight | Suitable Toyotas | Our comments |
|---|---|---|---|
| Box trailer, jet ski | 400-750 kg | Corolla Cross, C-HR, any larger model | Yaris Cross and Camry only to 400 kg |
| Camper trailer | 1,200-1,500 kg | RAV4 AWD, bZ4X AWD, Kluger, and up (not RAV4 2WD or bZ4X FWD) | RAV4 AWD at 1,500 kg leaves little margin; Kluger is more relaxed |
| Small/medium caravan | 2,000-2,500 kg | Fortuner, Prado, LandCruiser 300, HiLux 4x4 auto | Kluger only to 2,000 kg; check van's ATM, not tare |
| Large caravan, big boat | 2,500-3,500 kg | Prado, LandCruiser 300, HiLux 4x4 auto, Fortuner (to 3,100) | For regular towing above 3,000 kg, the LandCruiser 300's V6 is the most relaxed |

Always use the trailer's **ATM (aggregate trailer mass)**, which is its maximum loaded weight, not the tare weight shown in brochures.

## Trailer brakes and other legal points (NSW)

- Trailers over 750 kg loaded need brakes; over 2,000 kg they need brakes on all wheels and a breakaway system.
- Over 2,000 kg you will typically need an electric brake controller fitted in the vehicle (we fit Genuine or approved units).
- Towing mirrors are required when the trailer is wider than the vehicle's mirrors give clear view of.
