# RAV4: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Mid-size SUV. **Powertrain:** 2.5L hybrid, 2WD or AWD. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 1,500 kg (AWD only; 2WD about 800 kg).

The sixth-generation RAV4 arrived in Australia in early 2026. It keeps the hybrid-only approach of the previous model, updates the cabin with larger screens and the latest Toyota Safety Sense, and adds a broader grade walk. Plug-in hybrid versions (XSE and GR Sport PHEV, up to about 227 kW) and an XSE hybrid also exist in the national range; our configurator covers the four core hybrid grades, and the team can price the others on request.

## Who it suits

RAV4 is the default answer for a family of four or five that wants economy and space in one package. Choose AWD if you visit the Blue Mountains or south coast in winter, use gravel roads or plan to tow a small camper; AWD is also the only RAV4 rated to 1,500 kg.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** Large touchscreen with wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; Digital driver display; Adaptive cruise, lane trace, blind-spot monitor; Alloy wheels and LED headlights.

**GXL:** Adds dual-zone climate control; Wireless phone charger; Front parking sensors; Powered tailgate.

**Edge:** Rugged styling and all-terrain-biased tyres; Trail and Snow modes with downhill assist; Roof rails and distinctive rugged trim; Panoramic view monitor.

**Cruiser:** Leather-accented, heated front seats; Panoramic sunroof; Head-up display; 9-speaker JBL audio.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.
- **Roof & Cargo Pack** ($1,290 fitted): Genuine roof cross bars plus a lockable roof pod or cargo barrier (model dependent).

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense (latest generation) with AEB, lane trace, adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitor and driver monitoring; launched without an ANCAP rating; Toyota has said it expects a five-star result in late 2026.

## Buying notes

Demand for the new RAV4 is high, so factory-order lead times are longer than for most of the range (see dealer policy). Note that the official pre-launch list prices rose noticeably compared with the previous generation.
