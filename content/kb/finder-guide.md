# Which Toyota suits you? A buyer's finder guide

This guide maps common needs to models in the current (2026) Toyota range. Prices are Harbourline's illustrative NSW drive-away estimates from our configurator. When a buyer has several needs, work from the hardest constraint first: **towing weight, then seats, then off-road, then budget, then everything else.**

## By main use

### Commuting and city driving
- **Best fits:** Corolla, Yaris Cross, Corolla Cross, Camry, bZ4X.
- Hybrids use about 4 L/100 km in traffic, where they are most efficient. Camry suits long motorway commutes (M4, M2, M7) and rideshare. Yaris Cross and Corolla are easiest to park.
- With home charging and a daily drive under about 150 km, bZ4X typically has the lowest energy cost per kilometre in the range.

### Family of four
- **Best fits:** RAV4, Corolla Cross, Camry. RAV4 is the default recommendation: big boot, hybrid economy, AWD option.

### Family of five or more / seven seats
- **Seven seats:** Kluger (car-like, hybrid, tows 2 t), Prado 250 (4x4, tows 3.5 t), LandCruiser 300 GXL and above (4x4, tows 3.5 t), Fortuner (budget 4x4, tows 3.1 t).
- Family of five with three child seats across the back: Kluger and Prado are the most comfortable. RAV4 can work with narrow seats but check fitment.
- Kluger is the pick for families who do not tow heavy or go off-road; Prado if they do.

### Towing bands (braked, use the trailer's ATM)
| Load | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Up to 750 kg (box trailer, jet ski) | Corolla Cross, C-HR (725 kg), RAV4, bZ4X and up |
| 1,200-1,500 kg (camper trailer) | RAV4 AWD or bZ4X AWD (at their limit), Kluger (comfortable margin), any 4x4 |
| 2,000-2,500 kg (small/medium caravan) | Prado, Fortuner, HiLux 4x4 auto, LandCruiser 300; Kluger only to 2,000 kg |
| 2,500-3,500 kg (large van or boat) | LandCruiser 300 (most relaxed), Prado, HiLux 4x4 auto; Fortuner to 3,100 kg |
Remember GCM limits: at maximum towing you may not be able to carry a full payload. See the towing guide.

### Work and trade
- **HiLux** is the core choice: WorkMate for fleets and tight budgets, SR for 4x4 work use, SR5 for work-and-play. Cab-chassis versions take trays and service bodies.
- Seven seats plus tradie towing: Fortuner. Delivery and sales reps: Camry or Corolla.

### Off-road and touring
- **Serious 4x4 (low range, diff locks):** LandCruiser 300, Prado, Fortuner, HiLux 4x4.
- **Gravel roads and snow trips (no low range needed):** RAV4 AWD (Edge is the most rugged), Kluger, Corolla Cross AWD, bZ4X AWD.

### Electric readiness
- **Ready now (off-street parking, home charger possible):** bZ4X FWD (about 590 km WLTP) or AWD (1,500 kg towing).
- **Not ready for a full EV (apartment, street parking, long country trips):** a hybrid (any of Yaris Cross, Corolla, Corolla Cross, C-HR, Camry, RAV4, Kluger). Ask the team about RAV4 plug-in hybrid if you can charge at home but sometimes travel long distances.
- Novated lease buyers: an eligible EV such as bZ4X may be exempt from FBT.

### Enthusiasts
- GR86 (rear-drive coupe, from about $48,790), GR Yaris (rally-bred AWD hatch), GR Corolla (five-door AWD hot hatch). None are rated for towing.

## Quick decision rules

1. Tows over 2,000 kg: Prado, LandCruiser 300, HiLux 4x4 or Fortuner only.
2. Needs seven seats and tows under 2,000 kg: Kluger.
3. Needs seven seats and tows over 2,000 kg: Prado (or Fortuner on a budget, LandCruiser 300 for heavy vans).
4. Five seats, family, light towing: RAV4 (AWD if towing 800-1,500 kg).
5. Commuter on a budget: Corolla or Yaris Cross; long-distance commuter: Camry.
6. Can charge at home and wants to stop buying fuel: bZ4X.
7. Needs a ute: HiLux.
8. Wants a car in time for the summer school holidays (from 18 December 2026): prioritise ready-now stock, because RAV4, Prado and LandCruiser 300 factory orders are unlikely to arrive in time.
