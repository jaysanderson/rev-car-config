## Things to know

- **Payload and GVM:** heavy accessories (bull bar, canopy, drawers, roof racks) reduce the payload and the weight you can tow on a ute or 4x4. We will check this with you.
- **Driver-assist sensors:** bull bars and some front accessories must be Toyota-approved so radar and camera systems keep working correctly.
- **Finance:** accessories fitted before delivery can be included in the finance amount.
- **Timing:** most accessories are fitted before delivery; bull bars, canopies and tonneau covers can add a few days.
