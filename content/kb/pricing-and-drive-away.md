# How we build a drive-away price in NSW

All prices in the Harbourline configurator are **illustrative drive-away NSW estimates**. They are a guide only and are not an offer. Your written quote will show the exact figure for the vehicle you choose, on the day.

## What "drive-away" includes

A drive-away price is everything you need to legally drive the car away on the day:

| Component | What it is | Typical amount (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle list price | Manufacturer's list price before on-road costs, including GST and any luxury car tax | varies |
| Dealer delivery | Pre-delivery inspection, cleaning, fuel, handover, number plate fitting | about $2,190 in our estimates |
| Stamp duty (motor vehicle duty) | NSW Government duty on the vehicle's dutiable value | see formula below |
| Registration | 12 months NSW registration, plates and motor vehicle tax (weight-based) | about $690-$1,050 |
| CTP green slip | 12 months compulsory third party insurance | about $650 in our estimates (varies by insurer, postcode and driver age) |
| Premium paint | Any colour other than the standard solid white | about $675 |
| Options and accessories | Leather-accented trim upgrades, packs | as selected |

## NSW stamp duty

For passenger vehicles, NSW duty is calculated on the dutiable value (price including GST and dealer delivery, plus options):

- **Up to $45,000:** $3 for every $100 (or part).
- **Over $45,000:** $1,350 plus $5 for every $100 (or part) above $45,000.

Example: a dutiable value of $48,180 attracts $1,350 + (32 x $5) = $1,510 duty. NSW no longer gives a stamp duty exemption for electric vehicles. Different rules can apply for some commercial vehicles and for registered businesses; ask us.

## Worked example - RAV4 GX 2WD

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| List price before on-roads | $45,990 |
| Dealer delivery | $2,190 |
| Stamp duty | $1,510 |
| Registration (12 months) | about $820 |
| CTP (12 months) | about $650 |
| **Drive-away (rounded)** | **about $51,190** |

Add $675 for premium paint, and the cost of any options or packs.

## Luxury car tax

Luxury car tax (LCT) applies above a threshold set each financial year (for 2025-26: $80,567, or $91,387 for fuel-efficient vehicles). It is 33% of the amount above the threshold, and it is already built into the list prices of models such as LandCruiser 300 and upper-grade Prado.

## Premium paint

Glacier White is the no-cost colour on most Toyotas. Pearl, metallic and other premium paints are typically $675. On some models a two-tone roof or special paint costs more.

## Why our prices are labelled illustrative

- Toyota Australia sets list prices and can change them at any time; some 2026 list prices in our data are estimates where an official figure was not available to us.
- Registration and CTP vary by the vehicle's weight, your postcode, your age and your insurer.
- Ready-now stock can carry a bonus or discount (see dealer policy), and factory-order prices are the price on the day of delivery unless price protection applies.
- Fleet, government, ABN-holder and novated lease pricing can differ.
