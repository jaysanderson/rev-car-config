# Toyota Warranty Advantage

Every new Toyota sold by Harbourline comes with Toyota Warranty Advantage. These are the main terms as published by Toyota Australia; the Warranty and Service Book for your vehicle has the full conditions. The warranty is in addition to your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Summary

| Cover | Period | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| New vehicle warranty | 5 years, unlimited km | From delivery |
| Engine and driveline (including hybrid system) | Extended to 7 years, unlimited km | Vehicle serviced on schedule per the Warranty and Service Book |
| Hybrid battery | Up to 10 years, unlimited km | Annual hybrid health check from the year-5 service onwards |
| Perforation (rust-through) | 7 years | Regardless of servicing |
| Commercial use (taxi, rideshare as primary use, hire) | 5 years or 160,000 km, whichever first | Applies to vehicles used commercially |
| Genuine accessories fitted before delivery | Generally the same as the vehicle warranty | Check the accessory terms |

## Five-year new vehicle warranty

Covers repairs to correct defects in materials or workmanship for five years from delivery with no kilometre limit for private use. Wear-and-tear items (tyres, brake pads, wiper blades, bulbs) and damage from accidents, misuse or modifications are not covered.

## Seven-year engine and driveline extension

If the vehicle is serviced according to the schedule (at Harbourline, any Toyota dealer, or an independent repairer using parts and fluids to Toyota's specification and recording it in the logbook), cover for the engine and driveline is extended to seven years. Driveline includes the transmission, transfer case, differentials and the hybrid system's electrical drive components.

## Hybrid battery warranty - up to 10 years

The hybrid battery is covered for five years under the standard warranty. From the year-five service, an annual **hybrid health check** extends cover for a further year each time, up to 10 years from delivery, with no kilometre limit. The health check is included at no extra charge when done with a routine service at a Toyota dealer; a check outside a scheduled service may attract a labour charge.

## bZ4X battery

The bZ4X high-voltage battery has its own warranty terms, which are set out in the vehicle's Warranty and Service Book. Ask us for the current battery period and capacity-retention conditions before purchase.

## Commercial use

If a vehicle is used commercially (for example as a taxi or hire car), the warranty is limited to five years or 160,000 km. Rideshare and delivery use can affect cover; ask us if this applies to you.

## What you need to do

1. Service on time (time or kilometres, whichever comes first).
2. Keep the logbook or digital service record up to date.
3. For hybrids, keep doing the annual hybrid health check after year five.
4. Report faults promptly.
