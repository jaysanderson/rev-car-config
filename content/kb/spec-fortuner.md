# Fortuner: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Large 4x4 SUV. **Powertrain:** 2.8L turbo-diesel, part-time 4WD. **Seats:** 7. **Braked towing:** 3,100 kg.

Fortuner is a seven-seat wagon built on HiLux ute underpinnings. It is tougher and more off-road capable than a Kluger and cheaper than a Prado, with the same proven 2.8L diesel used across Toyota's work vehicles.

## Who it suits

It suits value-focused buyers who tow a mid-size caravan or boat, regional families, and people who want a simple diesel 4x4 with seven seats. The ride is firmer than Prado and the third row is tighter.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** Seven seats; Touchscreen with CarPlay/Android Auto; Rear diff lock; Adaptive cruise.

**GXL:** Adds keyless entry; Built-in sat-nav; Front sensors; 18-inch alloys.

**Crusade:** Leather-accented, powered front seats; Power tailgate; JBL audio; Panoramic view monitor.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.
- **Roof & Cargo Pack** ($1,290 fitted): Genuine roof cross bars plus a lockable roof pod or cargo barrier (model dependent).

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane departure alert, adaptive cruise; ANCAP 5 stars (2019 rating, now expired).

## Buying notes

The Fortuner is an older design than Prado; its safety rating has expired. Ask the team about ready-now stock, which often carries a run-out bonus.
