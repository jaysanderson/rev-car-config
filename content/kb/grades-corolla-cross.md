# Corolla Cross grades explained

Every Corolla Cross in our configurator is a 2.0L hybrid (146 kW combined). GX is 2WD only; GXL and Atmos can be ordered with AWD, which adds a rear electric motor. Braked towing is 750 kg across the range. Prices are Harbourline's illustrative NSW drive-away estimates for the 2WD version; list prices are indicative, following the 2026 facelift.

| Grade | Drive | List before on-roads (indicative) | Drive-away NSW (indicative) | Step from grade below |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GX | 2WD | about $37,290 | $42,190 | - |
| GXL | 2WD or AWD | about $41,690 | $46,690 | about +$4,500 |
| Atmos | 2WD or AWD | about $48,410 | $53,790 | about +$7,100 |

AWD typically adds about $3,000 to the list price.

## GX - the practical starter SUV

GX has a 10.5-inch touchscreen with wireless smartphone mirroring, reversing camera and rear sensors, 17-inch alloys, and full Toyota Safety Sense including adaptive cruise, lane trace assist and blind-spot monitoring.

**Who buys it:** first-time SUV buyers, young couples, and families on a firm budget who want more space than a Corolla.

## GXL - the all-rounder

Adds dual-zone climate control, smart keyless entry and push-button start, front parking sensors, built-in navigation and 18-inch alloys, and opens up the AWD option.

**Who buys it:** families who want AWD for wet weather and gravel, and buyers who like the convenience of keyless entry with a child on one hip.

## Atmos - the premium small SUV

Adds leather-accented, heated and powered front seats, a panoramic view monitor, powered tailgate, JBL audio and a panoramic sunroof.

**Who buys it:** downsizers from larger SUVs who don't want to give up comfort features; buyers comparing with premium-brand small SUVs.

## Practical notes

- Corolla Cross vs RAV4: Corolla Cross is cheaper and easier to park; RAV4 has more rear and boot space and tows up to 1,500 kg (AWD).
- Corolla Cross vs Yaris Cross: Corolla Cross is noticeably larger and stronger, and tows 750 kg vs 400 kg.
