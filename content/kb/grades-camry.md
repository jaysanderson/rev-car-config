# Camry grades explained

The current Camry (launched 2025) is hybrid-only. All three grades use the same 2.5L hybrid with 170 kW combined and front-wheel drive, and all return around 4 L/100 km. You are choosing equipment, not performance. Prices are Harbourline's illustrative NSW drive-away estimates; list prices are indicative.

| Grade | List before on-roads (indicative) | Drive-away NSW (indicative) | Step from grade below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ascent | about $40,490 | $45,490 | - |
| Ascent Sport | about $43,490 | $48,590 | about +$3,100 |
| SL | about $54,490 | $60,090 | about +$11,500 |

## Ascent - the well-equipped entry grade

Ascent already includes a 12.3-inch touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a 12.3-inch digital driver display, wireless phone charging, blind-spot monitor with rear cross-traffic alert, and the full Toyota Safety Sense suite including adaptive cruise and lane trace assist.

**Who buys it:** rideshare drivers, fleets and novated-lease buyers who want low running costs in a large car.

## Ascent Sport - more style, a little more kit

Adds sportier exterior styling, 17-inch alloys, dual-zone climate control, built-in navigation and interior ambient lighting.

**Who buys it:** private buyers who want the Camry to look less like a fleet car. Small step in price for a noticeable change in presentation.

## SL - the comfortable flagship

Adds leather-accented, heated and ventilated front seats, a head-up display, JBL premium audio, panoramic view monitor and a powered sunroof.

**Who buys it:** retirees and long-distance commuters who value comfort, and buyers stepping out of a European prestige sedan.

## Practical notes

- **Towing:** 400 kg braked, so only a light box or bike trailer.
- **Boot:** around 524 L, bigger than most mid-size SUVs.
- **Rideshare:** check the rideshare platform's current vehicle rules; Camry Ascent is often accepted on rideshare platforms.
- **Servicing:** capped-price servicing applies; see the service article.
