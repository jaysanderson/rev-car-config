# NSW public school terms and holidays - 2026

These are the NSW Department of Education dates for **public schools** in 2026. Catholic and independent schools often have slightly different dates, and schools near the Victorian and Queensland borders may follow other calendars. Families often time a new car around school holidays (for road trips, caravan holidays and delivery), so we keep these handy.

## 2026 terms (Eastern division, which covers Sydney)

| Term | Students start | Students finish |
|---|---|---|
| Term 1 | Monday 2 February 2026 | Thursday 2 April 2026 |
| Term 2 | Wednesday 22 April 2026 | Friday 3 July 2026 |
| Term 3 | Tuesday 21 July 2026 | Friday 25 September 2026 |
| Term 4 | Tuesday 13 October 2026 | Thursday 17 December 2026 |

Western division schools started Term 1 later, on Monday 9 February 2026; Terms 2-4 are the same.

## 2026 school holidays

| Holiday | Dates |
|---|---|
| Autumn (around Easter) | Tuesday 7 April to Friday 17 April 2026 (Good Friday 3 April and Easter Monday 6 April are public holidays immediately before) |
| Winter | Monday 6 July to Friday 17 July 2026 |
| Spring | Monday 28 September to Friday 9 October 2026 |
| Summer 2026-27 | Friday 18 December 2026 to Wednesday 27 January 2027 (Eastern division) |

Western division summer holidays run to Wednesday 3 February 2027.

## School development days (staff only)

Students do not attend on staff development days. In 2026 these fell before Term 1 (late January), on 20-21 April (start of Term 2), 20 July (start of Term 3) and 12 October (start of Term 4). Individual schools may schedule others.

- **Today (13 October 2026) is the first student day of Term 4.** The summer holidays start on Friday 18 December 2026, about 9 weeks away.
- **Ready-now stock** can be delivered within days, so it is the safe option if you need a car (and tow bar) for a summer road trip.
- **Factory orders** placed now for most hybrids (6-14 weeks) may or may not land before Christmas; RAV4, Prado and LandCruiser 300 orders are very unlikely to arrive before the summer break. See the dealer policy article for typical wait times.
- Tow bars, brake controllers and roof racks should be fitted at least a week before departure so you can do a short practice tow.
