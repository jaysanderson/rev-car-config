# Harbourline Used: how we sell pre-owned cars

Harbourline Toyota sells pre-owned cars at all four sites (Parramatta, Hornsby, Penrith and Liverpool). Any car can be viewed or delivered at the site closest to you; cars held at another site move on the Thursday (Hornsby, Liverpool) or Friday (Penrith) transfer runs. Figures in this prototype are illustrative demo data.

## Prices

Advertised used prices **exclude government charges**: NSW stamp duty, registration transfer and compulsory third-party insurance are added at contract. We quote a drive-away figure on request. Prices are reviewed weekly against the market data our business intelligence tool (Domo) publishes; a car priced "below market" on the listing is priced under the median asking price for the same model and model-year band.

## Toyota Certified Pre-Owned (illustrative programme terms)

A Toyota is offered as **Toyota Certified** when it is under 10 years old and under 160,000 km, has a verifiable service history, passes a **120-point inspection** by a Toyota-trained technician, and has a clear PPSR check. Certified cars come with:

- a **12-month Toyota Certified warranty** (unlimited kilometres) that starts on delivery, on top of any balance of the new-car warranty;
- **24/7 roadside assistance** for 12 months;
- a **7-day / 750 km exchange promise**: if the car is not right, swap it for another Harbourline car of equal or higher value within 7 days or 750 km, whichever comes first;
- a Toyota Health Check of the hybrid battery on every hybrid.

Cars that are not certified (older, higher kilometres, or non-Toyota trade-ins) are sold with a 90-point Harbourline inspection, a clear PPSR check and the statutory NSW dealer warranty (3 months or 5,000 km for cars under 10 years old and under 160,000 km).

## Balance of new-car warranty

Toyota's new-car warranty is 5 years, unlimited kilometres, from the first registration date; it transfers to the next owner. Engine and driveline cover extends to 7 years when the car is serviced to the Toyota schedule. The listing shows the date the balance runs to.

## Service history and what "logged service" means

A logged service is a scheduled service recorded in the service book or Toyota's national service record. Toyota's schedule is every 15,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first. The provenance panel shows each service with its date, odometer reading, the work done and who did it. Every car also receives a Harbourline pre-delivery service before sale.

## PPSR

Every car is checked on the Personal Property Securities Register before it is listed and again before delivery. We publish the check date and certificate number. "Encumbrance: none" means no finance is owed on the car; "written-off register: not listed" means the car has never been recorded as a repairable or statutory write-off in Australia.

## Reserve (48-hour hold)

"Reserve now" places a **48-hour hold** on a car so nobody else can buy it while you arrange a viewing, finance or a trade-in valuation. In this prototype no payment is taken; in production the hold is secured by a $1,000 refundable deposit. A held car shows as "Held" on the listing and is released automatically after 48 hours if the sale does not proceed.

## Enquiries and finance

An enquiry or finance enquiry from the website creates a lead in the dealer management system for the site that holds the car. A salesperson replies within one business hour during opening hours (Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm). Finance enquiries are indicative only: a quote requires a credit application, and terms from 24 to 84 months are available through Toyota Finance and partner lenders.

## Test drives and trade-ins

Any pre-owned car can be test-driven at the site that holds it or, with two days' notice, at your nearest Harbourline site. Bring your current car for an on-the-spot appraisal; a written valuation is valid for 7 days.

## Returns and cooling-off

NSW law gives private buyers a one-business-day cooling-off period on a dealer car purchase financed through the dealer; the Toyota Certified exchange promise above applies in addition to statutory rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
