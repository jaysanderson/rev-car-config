# LandCruiser Prado: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Large 4x4 SUV. **Powertrain:** 2.8L turbo-diesel with 48V assist, full-time 4WD. **Seats:** 7. **Braked towing:** 3,500 kg.

The 250 Series Prado launched in late 2024 and is the mainstay of Toyota's 4x4 wagon range. It rides on the same ladder-frame architecture family as the LandCruiser 300, with a 2.8L turbo-diesel that uses a small 48V system to smooth take-off and stop-start.

## Who it suits

Prado suits caravanners, outback tourers, families who regularly drive to the snow or beach tracks, and buyers who need seven seats and 3.5 t of towing in one vehicle. It is large: check your garage length and height before committing.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** 5 seats standard (7 optional); 12.3-inch touchscreen; Adaptive cruise and lane trace; Full-time 4WD with low range and centre diff lock.

**GXL:** Seven seats; Synthetic leather-accented trim; Panoramic view monitor; Front parking sensors and power tailgate glass.

**VX:** Leather-accented heated/ventilated front seats; Power tailgate; JBL audio; Wireless phone charger.

**Altitude:** Builds on VX equipment; Off-road-oriented styling and wheel package; Five or seven seats; Distinct exterior trim.

**Kakadu:** Adaptive variable suspension; Stabiliser disconnect for wheel articulation; Head-up display; Top-spec cabin finishes.
