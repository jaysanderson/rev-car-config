# Corolla Cross: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Small SUV. **Powertrain:** 2.0L hybrid, 2WD or AWD. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 750 kg.

Corolla Cross is the practical small SUV in the range: more rear-seat and boot space than a Corolla hatch, a strong 2.0L hybrid and the option of AWD. It received a facelift for the 2026 model year with updated styling and screens.

## Who it suits

It is a natural fit for young families, couples who want a higher seating position, and buyers moving out of an older RAV4-sized car who do not need the extra space. The 750 kg towing figure covers a box trailer, jet ski or small camper-style trailer, but not a camper trailer at 1.2 t or above.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** 10.5-inch touchscreen, wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; Adaptive cruise, lane trace and blind-spot monitoring; 17-inch alloys; Reversing camera and rear sensors.

**GXL:** Adds dual-zone climate control; Smart entry and push-button start; Front parking sensors; Built-in sat-nav; 18-inch alloys.

**Atmos:** Leather-accented, heated and powered front seats; Panoramic view monitor; Powered tailgate; JBL audio; Panoramic sunroof.
