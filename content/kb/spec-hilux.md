# HiLux: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Ute (single, extra or double cab). **Powertrain:** 2.8L turbo-diesel, 48V assist on autos, 4x2 or 4x4. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 3,500 kg (4x4 automatic; 4x2 WorkMate and manual variants less).

The updated HiLux went on sale in December 2025. The big change is that the 2.8L turbo-diesel is now standard across the range (no more 2.4L or petrol), with a 48V mild-hybrid assist on automatic versions. The range was trimmed to 16 variants, from the WorkMate up to the Rogue and the off-road Rugged X. A battery-electric HiLux in SR and SR5 double cab was also announced for 2026.

## Who it suits

HiLux suits tradies, farmers, fleet buyers, and lifestyle owners who tow boats, vans and camper trailers. WorkMate and SR are the work grades; SR5 is a common private choice; Rogue and Rugged X are the flagship options.

## Key equipment by grade

**WorkMate:** 2.8L diesel now standard; Touchscreen with smartphone mirroring; Hard-wearing vinyl/fabric cabin; Cab-chassis or pick-up.

**SR:** 48V assist on automatics; Larger touchscreen and digital cluster; Rear diff lock; Double cab pick-up.

**SR5:** Alloy wheels and LED headlights; Dual-zone climate; Built-in sat-nav; Smart entry.

**Rogue:** Leather-accented, heated front seats; Tub liner and sports bar; Panoramic view monitor; Premium audio.

**Rugged X:** Off-road focused flagship, priced alongside Rogue; Extra underbody protection; Off-road styling and all-terrain tyres; 48V assist automatic.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.
- **Roof & Cargo Pack** ($1,290 fitted): Genuine roof cross bars plus a lockable roof pod or cargo barrier (model dependent).

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace, adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitor (higher grades); ANCAP 5-star rating reported for the updated model.

## Buying notes

Rugged X sits alongside Rogue at the top of the range and is the choice for buyers who want factory off-road equipment rather than road-focused luxury. Payload falls as you add accessories, so check the GVM before fitting a canopy, bar work and a tow pack.
