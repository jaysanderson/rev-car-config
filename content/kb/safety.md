# Safety across the Toyota range

## Toyota Safety Sense (TSS)

Toyota Safety Sense is the name for Toyota's suite of camera- and radar-based driver-assistance features. Most current Toyotas use the third-generation or later system. Features vary by model and grade; the core items are:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Pre-Collision Safety (AEB) | Detects vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists (and on newer systems motorcycles) and brakes automatically if a crash is likely, day and night |
| Intersection turn assist / junction assist | Brakes for oncoming traffic or crossing pedestrians when turning at intersections (newer TSS) |
| Lane Trace Assist | Helps keep the car centred in its lane |
| Lane Departure Alert with steering assist | Warns and gently steers if you drift out of the lane |
| Adaptive cruise control (all-speed on most hybrids/automatics) | Keeps a set gap to the vehicle ahead |
| Road Sign Assist | Reads speed signs and shows them on the display |
| Automatic high beam | Switches high beam on and off |
| Emergency steering assist | Adds steering support during an evasive manoeuvre |
| Proactive driving assist (newer models) | Gentle braking and steering support in curves and when approaching slower traffic |

Other common safety features include blind-spot monitor, rear cross-traffic alert (with braking on some grades), safe exit assist, driver monitoring, panoramic view monitors and multiple airbags including front-centre airbags in newer models.

## ANCAP ratings (as at October 2026)

ANCAP ratings now carry a date stamp and expire six years after the rating year (on 31 December). An expired rating does not mean a car has become unsafe; it means the rating is no longer current under today's tougher tests.

| Model | ANCAP status |
|---|---|
| Yaris Cross | 5 stars (2021) |
| Corolla | 5 stars (2018) - expired |
| Corolla Cross | 5 stars (2022) |
| C-HR | 5 stars (2024) |

Always check ancap.com.au for the latest result before relying on it, particularly for fleet policies that require a current five-star rating.

## Which models have what

- **Newest TSS with proactive driving assist and driver monitoring:** RAV4 (2026), Camry, C-HR, Prado 250, HiLux (2026), bZ4X (2026).
- **Full TSS with lane trace and adaptive cruise:** Yaris Cross, Corolla, Corolla Cross, Kluger, LandCruiser 300.
- **Earlier system (lane departure alert rather than lane trace):** Fortuner.
- **GR86:** driver-assist features (AEB, adaptive cruise) on automatic versions only.
- **GR Yaris, GR Corolla:** AEB and adaptive cruise standard; performance focus means some comfort-assist features differ.
