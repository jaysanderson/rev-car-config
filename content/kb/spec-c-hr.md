# C-HR: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Small SUV (coupe-style). **Powertrain:** 1.8L or 2.0L hybrid, 2WD or AWD. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 725 kg.

The second-generation C-HR is the style choice among Toyota's small SUVs. It shares hybrid hardware with Corolla and Corolla Cross but wraps it in a sharper, coupe-like body with a high-tech cabin.

## Who it suits

It suits singles and couples, and buyers who value design and technology over maximum rear space. The sloping roof and small rear windows make the back seat feel tighter than in a Corolla Cross, so bring the family along for a sit test if they will ride in the back often.

## Key equipment by grade

**GXL:** 12.3-inch touchscreen and digital cluster; Wireless phone charging; Adaptive cruise and lane trace; 18-inch alloys.

**Koba:** Adds 2.0L hybrid; Leather-accented heated seats; Head-up display; JBL audio; Powered tailgate.

**GR Sport:** AWD hybrid with sports tune; GR-styled body kit and 20-inch alloys; Sports seats; Panoramic view monitor.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace, blind-spot monitor, safe exit assist; ANCAP 5 stars (2024).

## Buying notes

All three grades are hybrid. The GR Sport is the only AWD C-HR and has a sport-focused tune and styling, not a higher-output engine in the GR Yaris sense.
