# GR Yaris: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Performance hatch. **Powertrain:** 1.6L turbo 3-cylinder, GR-Four AWD. **Seats:** 4. **Braked towing:** not rated.

The GR Yaris is a rally-bred hot hatch built in limited volume by Toyota's Gazoo Racing division. The 2025 update added more power, a new eight-speed automatic option and a revised cockpit. It shares little with the regular Yaris beyond the name.

## Who it suits

It suits enthusiasts, track-day drivers and collectors. It is firm, noisy and compact; it is not a practical family car and has no towing rating.

**GTS:** Adds Torsen front and rear limited-slip diffs; Forged BBS-style alloys; Suede/leather-accented sports seats; Track-focused suspension.
