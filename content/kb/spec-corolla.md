# Corolla: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Small hatch / sedan. **Powertrain:** 1.8L hybrid, front-wheel drive. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** not rated.

The Corolla is Toyota's small car and is now sold in Australia as a hybrid only. It comes as a hatch or a sedan. For 2026 the ZR sedan has been dropped; the ZR is hatch-only, while the Ascent Sport and SX are offered in both body styles.

## Who it suits

Corolla suits commuters, students, rideshare starters and anyone wanting a low-cost, low-fuss car. The sedan has a much bigger boot than the hatch and is the better pick if you carry luggage or a pram. The hatch is easier to park and feels a little sportier.

## Key equipment by grade

**Ascent Sport:** 12.3-inch digital driver display; Wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; Adaptive cruise and lane trace assist; 16-inch alloys.

**SX:** Adds 18-inch alloys (hatch); Built-in sat-nav; Dual-zone climate control; Wireless phone charger.

**ZR:** Synthetic leather and suede-style sports seats; Head-up display; JBL premium audio; Heated front seats; Adaptive LED headlights.

## Accessory packs

- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace assist, adaptive cruise, road sign assist; ANCAP 5 stars (2018 rating, now expired).

## Buying notes

The hybrid Corolla is not a towing car; if towing matters, step to Corolla Cross (750 kg) or RAV4. The GR Corolla is a separate performance model with its own article.
