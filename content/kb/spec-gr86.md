# GR86: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Sports coupe. **Powertrain:** 2.4L flat-four petrol, rear-wheel drive. **Seats:** 4. **Braked towing:** not rated.

The GR86 is a light, rear-wheel-drive coupe co-developed with Subaru. It is the most affordable GR model and is prized for balance and steering feel rather than outright speed.

## Who it suits

It suits weekend drivers, driving-school graduates moving up, and enthusiasts who want a manual rear-driver with low running costs for its class. Rear seats are occasional only.

## Key equipment by grade

**GT:** 2.4L boxer, 174 kW; Limited-slip differential; 8-inch touchscreen with CarPlay/Android Auto; 17-inch alloys.

**GTS:** Adds 18-inch alloys and sportier tyres; Suede/leather-accented heated seats; Rear spoiler; Aluminium pedals.
