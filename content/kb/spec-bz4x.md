# bZ4X: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Mid-size electric SUV. **Powertrain:** Battery electric, FWD or dual-motor AWD. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 1,500 kg (AWD only; FWD 750 kg).

The bZ4X was substantially upgraded for 2026 with a larger 74.7 kWh battery, more powerful motors, faster AC charging and a lower price than the original. A larger Touring wagon flagship (about 280 kW, from about $69,990 before on-roads) joined the range in mid-2026; it is not in our configurator but we can quote it.

## Who it suits

bZ4X suits households with off-street parking and home charging, commuters doing 30-100 km a day, and buyers who want a Toyota-backed EV with the same dealer network for servicing. The AWD's 1,500 kg towing rating covers a small camper trailer, although range drops sharply when towing.

## Key equipment by grade

**FWD:** 74.7 kWh battery, around 590 km WLTP; 22 kW AC charging capability; Large touchscreen with wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; Heated front seats.

**AWD:** Dual motors, around 250 kW; 1,500 kg braked towing; Synthetic leather-accented trim; X-Mode and grip control for loose surfaces.

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB, lane trace, adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitor, safe exit assist; ANCAP 5 stars (2022).

## Buying notes

Toyota and dealers have run finance and charger offers on bZ4X during 2025-26; ask the team what applies today. NSW no longer offers an EV stamp duty exemption, so standard duty is included in the drive-away estimate.
