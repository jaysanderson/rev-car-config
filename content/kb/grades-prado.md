# LandCruiser Prado (250 Series) grades explained

Every Prado 250 uses the same 2.8L turbo-diesel with 48V assist (150 kW / 500 Nm), an eight-speed automatic and full-time 4WD with low range. All grades are rated to tow 3,500 kg braked. ## GX - the working Prado

The GX is the no-frills 4x4: cloth seats, hard-wearing finishes, a 12.3-inch touchscreen, adaptive cruise and lane trace assist. It has the same drivetrain and towing ability as the flagship.

**Who buys it:** rural and fleet buyers, people planning to fit their own touring accessories (bar work, drawers, dual battery), and families who just need the capability.

## GXL - the family tourer

GXL makes seven seats standard (a five-seat version exists) and adds synthetic leather-accented trim, a panoramic view monitor, front sensors and more convenience features. It is a popular choice for families.

**Who buys it:** families who tow a caravan on holidays and use the car as a daily driver the rest of the year.

## VX - comfort upgrade

VX adds genuine leather-accented, heated and ventilated front seats, a power tailgate, a JBL sound system and a wireless charger. Black or saddle tan leather-accented trim can be chosen from VX upwards.

**Who buys it:** grey nomads and long-distance tourers who spend whole days in the seat.

## Altitude - the adventure look

Altitude builds on VX with an off-road-oriented styling and wheel package and distinct exterior trim, and is offered in five- or seven-seat form.

**Who buys it:** buyers who want a premium Prado that looks the part on the track.

## Kakadu - the flagship

Kakadu adds adaptive variable suspension, a stabiliser disconnect system that improves wheel articulation off-road, a head-up display and the most premium cabin finishes.

**Who buys it:** those who want every comfort feature and adaptive suspension for on-road ride together with extra off-road articulation.

## Practical notes

- Prado is long and tall (about 4.99 m long, over 1.9 m high). Check home garage and apartment car-park clearances.
- Factory-order waits are among the longest in the range; ready-now stock across our four sites is the quickest option.
- For a 2.5-3.5 t caravan, Prado is capable, but buyers regularly towing near 3.5 t often prefer the LandCruiser 300's larger V6.
