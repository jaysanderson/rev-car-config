# Yaris Cross: specifications, grades and prices

**Type:** Small SUV. **Powertrain:** 1.5L hybrid, 2WD or AWD-i. **Seats:** 5. **Braked towing:** 400 kg.

The Yaris Cross is the entry point to Toyota's SUV range. It is based on the Yaris hatch but sits higher, has a more useful boot and comes only as a hybrid, so day-to-day fuel costs are low.

## Who it suits

It suits city commuters, first-car buyers, downsizers and anyone who parks on the street in tight inner-west or Parramatta streets. It is not a towing vehicle: the 400 kg rating covers a small box trailer or bike trailer only. Rear seat space is fine for children and shorter adults; families who need a pram plus shopping regularly should also look at the Corolla Cross.

## Key equipment by grade

**GX:** 8-inch touchscreen, wireless CarPlay/Android Auto; Adaptive cruise and lane trace assist; 16-inch alloys; Reversing camera.

**GXL:** Adds 10.5-inch touchscreen with sat-nav; Front and rear parking sensors; Blind-spot monitor and rear cross-traffic alert; Smart entry and push-button start.

**Urban:** Synthetic leather-accented seats; 18-inch alloys; Head-up display; Powered tailgate; Premium cabin trim.

## Accessory packs

- **Towing Pack** ($1,650 fitted): Genuine tow bar and tongue, wiring harness and trailer plug, fitted and rated to the vehicle's braked capacity.
- **Protection Pack** ($890 fitted): All-weather floor mats, cargo liner, bonnet protector and paint/interior protection treatment.
- **Roof & Cargo Pack** ($1,290 fitted): Genuine roof cross bars plus a lockable roof pod or cargo barrier (model dependent).

## Safety

Toyota Safety Sense with AEB (intersection turn assist), lane trace assist, adaptive cruise; ANCAP 5 stars (2021 rating).

## Buying notes

AWD-i adds a small electric motor on the rear axle for slippery driveways and gravel; it is not an off-road system. Hybrid battery warranty can extend to 10 years with annual health checks (see warranty article).
