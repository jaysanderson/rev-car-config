# HiLux grades explained (2026 update)

The 2026 HiLux (on sale from December 2025) uses a 2.8L turbo-diesel in every grade, with a 48V mild-hybrid assist on automatic versions. The range was simplified to 16 variants. Prices below are the cheapest version of each grade (body style and drivetrain affect price) and Harbourline's illustrative NSW drive-away estimates (indicative).

| Grade | Typical body | List from (before on-roads) | Drive-away NSW est. | Step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WorkMate | 4x2 single cab chassis, up to 4x4 double cab | $33,990 | $38,990 | - |
| SR | 4x4 extra or double cab | $52,990 | $58,790 | big step: 4x4 and higher spec |
| SR5 | 4x4 double cab pick-up (48V auto about $65,990) | $63,990 | $70,290 | about +$11,000 |
| Rogue | 4x4 double cab pick-up, 48V auto | $71,990 | $78,690 | about +$8,000 |
| Rugged X | 4x4 double cab pick-up, 48V auto | $71,990 | $78,690 | same price as Rogue |

Note: the WorkMate entry price is for a 4x2 single cab chassis; a WorkMate 4x4 double cab costs considerably more. Always compare like body styles.

## WorkMate - the tool

Hard-wearing cabin, smartphone mirroring, the 2.8 diesel (now standard, replacing the old 2.4 and petrol) and the choice of cab-chassis or pick-up tub. **Buyers:** tradies, councils, fleets and farms.

## SR - work-ready 4x4

Adds a larger screen and digital cluster, extra driver assistance, a rear diff lock on 4x4s and 48V assist on automatics. **Buyers:** trades and farmers who also use the ute on weekends and want a 4x4 double cab without the SR5's extras.

## SR5 - the private buyer's HiLux

Adds alloy wheels, LED headlights, dual-zone climate control, built-in navigation and smart entry. **Buyers:** families and lifestyle owners who tow boats or camper trailers. It is a common choice for private buyers.

## Rogue - premium road-focused flagship

Adds leather-accented, heated front seats, a tub liner and sports bar, panoramic view monitor and upgraded audio. **Buyers:** owners who want a ute with SUV-like comfort.

## Rugged X - off-road focused flagship

Rugged X sits at the same price as Rogue and focuses on factory off-road equipment rather than road-focused extras, including extra underbody protection, off-road styling and all-terrain tyres, with the 48V-assisted automatic. **Buyers:** owners who head off the bitumen regularly, tourers and outback travellers who want capability from the factory rather than aftermarket add-ons.

## Practical notes

- **Towing:** 4x4 automatics are rated up to 3,500 kg braked. Some 4x2 and manual variants are rated lower. Check GCM: at full towing capacity you cannot also carry a full payload.
- **Payload:** about 715-1,550 kg depending on variant. Bar work, canopy and tow pack all reduce it.
- **Electric HiLux:** a battery-electric HiLux in SR and SR5 double cab was announced for 2026; ask for current availability.
