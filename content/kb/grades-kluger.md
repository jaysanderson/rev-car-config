# Kluger grades explained

All Kluger grades share the same 2.5L AWD hybrid powertrain (184 kW combined), seven seats and a 2,000 kg braked towing capacity. The choice is purely about comfort and convenience equipment. Prices are Harbourline's illustrative NSW drive-away estimates.

| Grade | List (before on-roads) | Drive-away NSW est. | Step from grade below |
|---|---|---|---|
| GX | $62,410 | $68,690 | - |
| GXL | about $71,990 | $78,690 | about +$10,000 |
| Grande | $85,135 | $92,590 | about +$13,900 |

## GX - seven seats on a budget

The GX gives you everything a family needs: three rows with tri-zone climate control, a touchscreen with smartphone mirroring, alloy wheels, and the full Toyota Safety Sense suite with adaptive cruise and lane trace assist. Seat trim is cloth, which many parents prefer with young children.

**Who buys it:** larger families who want the hybrid economy and the seven seats without paying for luxuries; fleet buyers needing a people mover; grandparents doing regular school pick-ups.

## GXL - the sweet spot

GXL adds leather-accented and heated front seats, a powered tailgate, built-in navigation, smart keyless entry and extra convenience touches. The powered tailgate and easy-clean leather-accented trim are things families use every day.

**Who buys it:** families who will keep the car eight to ten years and want it to feel well specified for the whole time; buyers towing a camper who appreciate the extra comfort on long trips.

**Price step:** about $9,500-$10,000 list over GX.

## Grande - the luxury Kluger

Grande adds heated and ventilated front seats, a panoramic sunroof, a head-up display, a JBL premium audio system, larger 20-inch alloys and extra driver-assist and camera features. It feels genuinely upmarket inside.

**Who buys it:** buyers comparing Kluger with luxury-brand seven-seaters, executives on novated leases, and families who do frequent long road trips.

**Price step:** about $13,000 list over GXL.

## Practical notes

- **Towing:** every Kluger is rated to 2,000 kg braked with a maximum tow ball download of around 200 kg (10%). That covers most camper trailers and small single-axle caravans. For larger vans, look at Prado, Fortuner or LandCruiser 300.
- **Third row:** comfortable for children and teens; adults will manage short trips.
- **Fuel:** about 5.6 L/100 km combined is a big saving over the old V6; most owners see 6-7 L/100 km in mixed Sydney driving.
- **Boot:** small with all seven seats up (about 241 L); fold the third row for a large, flat area.
