# Track: OEMs selling direct or near-direct online

Researched 16 October 2026 for the Revolution Software Toyota dealer configurator prototype. Method: live OEM sites, opened in a browser (from Australia) or with a fetch tool (from the US), plus recent reporting. US-only OEM sites (toyota.com/SmartPath, bmwusa.com, fordblueadvantage.com, hyundaiusa.com) were geo-blocked, timed out or returned empty shells. For those I relied on OEM press releases and dealer-hosted program pages, and say so where it matters. Interactive configurators (Tesla US, Rivian) render to canvas, so I could not read their text. Configurator behaviour comes from what the page did expose and from reporting. Anything I could not check is marked **unverified**.

Market key: **[AU]** available in Australia, **[US]** US only, **[EU]** Europe.

## Tesla

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Discover / choose:** You book a test drive online ("walk-in availability is limited"). The AU site calls it a test drive and the US site calls it a demo drive. A "Compare Models" link sits inside the configurator. There is no needs-based quiz: model choice is the guidance. [AU][US]
- **Configure:** The AU Model Y Design page (opened) is a single page. It lists four variants with prices (Premium RWD $58,900, Premium LR AWD $68,900, Model Y L $74,900, Performance $84,900), with paint, wheels and interior marked "Included". FSD (Supervised) is offered as a $149/mo subscription, and charging hardware is an add-on (Home Charger $800, Mobile Charger $600). The header shows **"Est. Delivery: Oct – Nov 2026"** and live range, top speed and 0–100 figures for the selected variant. The US page would not render as text.
- **Price / finance:** Payment tabs at the top of the configurator (AU: **Cash / Novated Lease / Loan**) switch the whole page between a cash price and a payment view. The US destination fee is "non-negotiable". Sales tax and registration appear only near delivery, in the app. [AU][US]
- **Trade-in:** You can get an estimate before ordering. After ordering, in the app: Pre-delivery tasks → Trade-In → self-inspection. Tesla's AU FAQ says an Estimated Offer is valid for 7 days (search snippet only; I did not open that page).
- **Reserve / buy:** A **one-time, non-refundable order fee** by credit card. It was $100 in 2019, when it replaced a $1,000 refundable deposit, and rose to $250 in August 2021 (Electrek). I did not confirm the current US amount on the live configurator. You can edit the design ("Edit Design") until a VIN is assigned, but you cannot change the model. You cancel in the app under Manage Order. Credit cards are not accepted for US final payment. In AU, final payment is due 48 hours before delivery by bank transfer, or by credit card with a surcharge, in person via chip and PIN.
- **Deliver:** The app is "the source of truth" for timing. An SMS arrives when a VIN is assigned, then a scheduling link within days. You can reschedule in the app. Lease and finance contracts are e-signed in the app, and an MVPA is provided for outside lenders. In AU, Tesla handles registration for you in every state except the NT. Live **Virtual Education Sessions** are offered before handover.
- **Own:** The phone app is the key. You get software updates over the air, and service is booked through the app or the touchscreen.

**(b) Standout:** one account and one app from order to ownership, with a pre-delivery task checklist; price and estimated delivery shown in the configurator header; finance mode as a top-level toggle; registration done for you in AU; you can edit the configuration after ordering.

**(c) Numbers:** Consumer Reports 2026 owner survey: 69% would consider a Tesla again (4th), down from 91% "buy again" in 2016 (Jalopnik, May 2026). I found no published share of sales made online, conversion rate or NPS.

**(d) Weaknesses:** delivery-day defects and service capacity. I found long-running reports of paint and panel issues, but could not open a credible 2025 source (Motor1 returned 502). The order fee is non-refundable. Tax and registration costs show up late. The 7-day return policy was dropped in October 2020 (Electrek).

**(e) Dealers:** bypassed entirely, with company-owned stores and delivery centres.

## Rivian [US/Canada only; not sold in AU]

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Discover / choose:** Rivian Spaces and Demo Centers. R2 demo drives at selected Spaces were planned from about July 2026 (evwire). A December 2025 offer gave 500 Rivian Rewards points for a demo drive or trade-in quote plus a same-day order (offer page, now closed).
- **Configure:** The R2 configurator opened 15 May 2026. Only the Performance with Launch Package ($57,990) could be ordered, with other trims shown as previews. **Ordering is by invitation**: invitations go out in rolling batches weighted by reservation date and delivery location, and existing owners get faster timing. Lease-expiry dates can be added to your account to influence timing. Reservations are a $100 refundable deposit (Repairer Driven News, June 2026). Sources disagree on R2 Premium pricing ($48,490 versus $53,990).
- **Finance / trade-in / buy:** a **Rivian Guide** contacts you as delivery approaches. You then finalise payment, apply for finance, trade in your car and sign documents in your Rivian Account (support page, opened).
- **Deliver:** pickup at a service centre or home delivery "where available". The app **tracks the vehicle's journey on delivery day**, and you set up a digital key when it arrives. You can have a Field Specialist walkthrough or explore on your own.
- **Own:** app, digital key, software updates over the air, mobile service. The CEO claims service lead times have been cut from 20–40 days to 2–3 days in some markets (I could not open the article).
- **Rivian Shop / gear:** **unverified**. I could not render the shop pages.

**(b) Standout:** a named human Guide on top of a self-serve account; live delivery tracking in the app; invitation-based allocation that is open about why you are waiting.

**(c) Numbers:** Consumer Reports owner satisfaction is #1 for the third straight year, with 85% who would buy again (R1T 91%, R1S 79%) (Jalopnik, May 2026).

**(d) Weaknesses:** service capacity and coverage gaps, which the CEO acknowledged. Reliability scores are poor (reported, not verified). The configurator only lets you order to the extent you have an invitation.

**(e) Dealers:** bypassed.

## Polestar [AU primary; also EU/US]

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Discover:** Polestar Spaces (12 in AU, plus 19 service points) for viewing and test drives. A Germany page lists three paths: build in the configurator, buy a pre-configured car ready for faster delivery, or buy pre-owned.
- **Configure / price:** an online configurator with national pricing. When you choose a loan or lease, some Extras are blocked because lenders cannot finance them. Extras are sold through a separate webshop and fitted by dealers.
- **Buy:** a **Polestar ID is created automatically** at order and must be activated within 30 days. An order page then walks you through confirming the finance method and Extras → e-signing the contract (sent digitally, within about 10 minutes) → payment instructions → handover time. In AU you can cancel free within 14 days. The VIN appears on the order page when it is assigned. The spec is generally locked after confirmation, and changing it voids any finance application (AU Orders FAQ, opened).
- **Deliver:** you get an estimated delivery time at order, and it updates continuously on the order page and in the app. A suggested handover slot appears as delivery approaches. You choose the handover location during ordering. Home delivery is offered in Germany but not mentioned in the AU FAQ. You must collect in person for ID and registration checks.
- **Own:** app with order tracking, car controls and software update tracking.

**(b) Standout:** the order page is a status tracker with each next action shown (finance → e-sign → pay → handover); a 14-day free cancellation; the delivery estimate is live from the moment you order.

**(c) Numbers:** AU sales were 1,713 in 2024 (down 30.3%) and 1,631 year to date at September 2025 (up 46%). Global sales were up 51% in 2025. Q2 2025 loss was US$1bn (GoAuto, September 2025).

**(d) Weaknesses:** too dependent on fleet and rental volume (including Uber-related fleets in AU). Heavy discounting in 2025. A May 2024 report said AU would drop fixed pricing (EFTM).

**(e) Dealers:** **retreat from pure online**. The CEO calls it a "renaissance of the dealers": mostly Volvo retailers now hold sales responsibility. AU keeps a hybrid agency model in which Polestar owns the stock "at least in the short term".

## Ford US

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Discover / configure:** Build & Price and dealer inventory search on ford.com. Each vehicle has its own inventory page, by VIN. **Unverified:** I could not read ford.com's configurator.
- **Price:** under the Model e programme, certified EV dealers must **post one non-negotiable price on Ford.com, honoured in store** and audited by Ford. Prices can still differ between dealers, so customers cross-shop them. Buyers could purchase online and get remote delivery from January 2024, with regional replenishment centres targeting delivery in under 10 days (Electrek, May 2023). I could not confirm the 2026 status.
- **Order tracking:** the **Vehicle Order Tracking** tool asks for an order number and a VIN, both from the dealer. Orders are scheduled oldest first, subject to production and supplier capacity. Once a VIN is assigned, "no changes can be made". Online orders appear under My Account → Check Order Status, and dealers can see them too (Ford support page, opened). Owners describe stages such as Scheduled → In Production → Built → Shipped → Final Preparation and report mismatches between tracker and account (forums).
- **Used (Ford Blue Advantage):** three tiers. Gold: ≤6 years, <80k miles, 172-point inspection, 12-month/12k comprehensive and 7-year/100k powertrain warranty. EV: 127-point inspection, 8-year/100k BaseCARE EV warranty. Blue: any make, ≤10 years, <150k miles, 139-point inspection, 90-day/4k warranty. All tiers have a **14-day/1,000-mile money-back guarantee** handled through the selling dealer. Blue Advantage CPO is listed on **Amazon Autos** in LA, Seattle and Dallas, with itemised pricing within 75 miles, most paperwork done online, and dealer pickup. About 20 dealers were live out of 160–180 interested (Ford, November 2025).
- **Own:** FordPass, now the Ford app (connected services). **Unverified:** I found no source showing order tracking inside the app.

**(b) Standout:** a binding online price for EVs; order tracking down to production week; a cross-brand certified used programme with a 14-day return.

**(c) Numbers:** 1,920 of 2,968 dealers (about 65%) certified for Model e (December 2022).

**(d) Weaknesses:** tracking needs both an order number and a VIN, and the tracker and account don't always agree; the experience varies from dealer to dealer.

**(e) Dealers:** **dealer-fulfilled**, with Ford setting price and process rules. This is not direct sales.

## Toyota US (with Toyota Australia comparison)

**(a) Stage by stage, US SmartPath** (toyota.com and SmartPath sites were geo-blocked; details below are from Toyota press releases)
- **Discover / price:** real-time dealer inventory and real-time pricing. Pricing reaches dealer sites "within minutes", where third parties update overnight. Payments are shown with local taxes and fees. Research done online (averaging 18 hours per buyer) is **honoured in store**, at the same price.
- **Finance:** direct integration with Toyota Financial Services (TFS) to lease, finance or buy outright. Online F&I products were added in 2021.
- **Buy:** the **"digital garage"** saves your progress so you can start online and pick up where you left off at the dealer. Alternatively you can finish entirely online through a **digital appointment** with a SmartPath dealer.
- **Dealer integration:** one system online and in store, with no re-keying. It connects to the dealer's DMS (Real-Time Interface to CDK, Reynolds, Dealertrack, Tekion, PBS, Dominion and others), CRM (VinSolutions, eLead and others) and websites (Dealer.com, Dealer Inspire, DealerOn and others). It was extended to certified used cars (2021) and to service ("SmartPath for Service").
- **Trade-in, deposit, delivery mechanics:** **unverified**. These were not described in any Toyota source I could open.

**AU comparison (dealer "Order Online", opened live):** four steps: Vehicle → Details → Payment (cash or finance) → Summary. You pay a **$1,000 holding deposit** online ("pay the rest at the dealership"). The page shows the **total drive-away price** (RAV4 GX Hybrid 2WD $50,928) and asks about accessories, which only triggers a callback. A Toyota Finance application can be started online. No trade-in step was visible. Toyota says the order takes about 10 minutes.

**(b) Standout:** OEM-built software that dealers run; the online price carries into the store; deep DMS, CRM and website integration.

**(c) Numbers:** launched April 2020; 46,000+ sales and 150,000+ inquiries by December 2021; 165 dealers in March 2022; **1 millionth SmartPath purchase on 19 August 2025** (Toyota, September 2025). About 1,500 US dealers in total.

**(d) Weaknesses:** in shortage years, forum buyers reported dealers ignoring advertised prices or adding markups after a SmartPath start (anecdotal). The experience depends on how each dealer sets it up.

**(e) Dealers:** hybrid, with the **dealer at the centre**. Toyota supplies the platform, and the dealer owns the deal, price and profit.

## Hyundai

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Click to Buy / Shopper Assurance [US]** (program text from a participating dealer's page; hyundaiusa.com rendered empty): **Transparent Pricing**, with dealer prices posted online; **Click to Buy**, with payment calculator, online trade-in estimate (KBB, Black Book or Edmunds) and secure credit application, through Hyundai Motor Finance plus 2,000+ lenders; **Flexible Test Drive**, with the car brought to your home or work, scheduled in the Hyundai Drive app or on the dealer site; **3-Day Worry-Free Exchange**, under 300 miles, like-new and lien-free, exchanged for another new Hyundai by mutual agreement. This is an exchange, not a refund.
- **Amazon Autos [US]:** launched December 2024 with Hyundai as the exclusive launch brand: more than 200 dealers across 48 metro areas (Hyundai says 54 markets and 71% of its volume). Flow: search local dealer stock → firm trade-in value from an independent third party → fixed price "inclusive of all taxes and fees" → finance or pay in full (no leasing at launch) → e-sign → choose a pickup slot at the dealer. It has since grown to 130+ cities and added used and CPO cars (3-day/300-mile return, 30-day/1,000-mile warranty), starting with Hyundai dealers in LA.
- **AU:** Click to Buy historically let you reserve with a deposit, showed estimated drive-away pricing, and let you make offers dealers didn't have to accept. The first IONIQ 5 online allocation (2021) sold 240 units in two hours. **On 16 October 2026 hyundai.com/au/en/click-to-buy redirected to Find a Dealer**, so the AU online purchase path appears to have been withdrawn. I could not confirm this.
- **Own:** Hyundai app; myHyundaiCare in AU (7-year warranty conditional on dealer servicing, capped-price online service quotes).

**(b) Standout:** an Amazon checkout with all-in pricing and pickup booking; at-home test drives; a 3-day exchange.

**(c) Numbers:** coverage figures above. I found no published online share for Hyundai Motor America.

**(d) Weaknesses:** Shopper Assurance only works at participating dealers, and the exchange depends on the dealer agreeing; Amazon had no leasing at launch.

**(e) Dealers:** **dealer-fulfilled**. Amazon and Hyundai provide the front end, and the local dealer sells, delivers and services.

## Genesis

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **US Genesis Concierge (since May 2020):** a dedicated personal shopper by phone, text or email; **test drives "starting at your front door"** in many markets; paperwork delivered to your home, and the vehicle itself where permitted; the concierge stays your contact after the sale. The concierge coordinates with the local retailer. Service Valet (pickup and return for maintenance) is used by 90% of owners, according to Genesis via KBB.
- **AU (launched 2019):** company-owned Studios, fixed price, online build-and-order. **Genesis To You** covered demo cars brought to you (within about 70 km of city centres), handover delivery and service pickup. A named **Genesis Experience Executive** was your contact from first look through ownership. The 5-year ownership package bundled warranty, servicing, roadside assistance, map updates, Connected Services and valet.

**(b) Standout:** one named human for the whole relationship; the test drive, handover and service all come to you.

**(c) Numbers:** the J.D. Power 2025 Tech Experience Index ranked Genesis highest overall for a fifth year (538) (search; not opened). AU delivered 1,602 vehicles last year, now about 150 a month (CarsGuide, July 2026).

**(d) Weaknesses:** in AU the direct model left the brand "niche", with no presence in Adelaide and thin service coverage.

**(e) Dealers:** **AU is moving "from a direct model to an agency model"** with dealer-owned agencies (CEO Don Romano, July 2026). In the US, retailers are kept and the concierge sits on top.

## BMW

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **AU (August 2025):** upgraded configurator with 360° views, zoom, BMW Individual paints, an upholstery choice reflected in the exterior render, a **headlight and taillight on/off toggle**, and the ability to "specify and order" online. A **real-time order tracking portal** follows the car from factory to dealer, including while it is on the ship. A separate lifestyle store sells merchandise for dealer pickup or home delivery.
- **US "Buy Online":** I could not open it (timed out). Search results say it runs only at participating dealers: **unverified**.
- **UK BMW Retail Online (2015, the reference implementation):** a four-question "Find your BMW" needs quiz; Genius live chat 8am–10pm; full configuration or pre-built cars with faster availability; a finance calculator; a credit decision within 90 seconds; a retailer online showroom for chat, test drive and part-exchange valuation; you could pay a deposit or the full amount online, in "less than 10 minutes". 95% of UK retailers opted in.
- **EU agency:** MINI's agency model runs in 24 European markets. The core BMW brand moves to agency in **mid-2027** (delayed from 2026), with national fixed prices and a fixed commission per car for dealers. Customers can move between online and retailer at any step. BMW calls AI "the foundation of the entire sales system" (BMWBLOG, October 2026). BMW said there are no agency plans for Australia (search; not opened).

**(b) Standout:** a needs-based quiz (UK); visual detail in the configurator; factory-to-dealer tracking that includes the ship (AU).

**(c) Numbers:** a 2023 forecast put about 25% of BMW orders online without a salesperson (search; not opened). UK buyers made 1.4 dealer visits before purchase (2015).

**(d) Weaknesses:** agency rollout delayed twice; in AU, ordering ends at the dealer.

**(e) Dealers:** hybrid. In the EU, dealers become paid agents. In AU and the US, the franchise stays.

## Mercedes-Benz

**(a) Stage by stage, AU Mercedes-Benz Store (opened)**
- Browse **all national stock** and online-exclusive pre-orders of launch models. A "Star of the Month" collection is available online only.
- Flow: choose vehicle → payment method (outright, or Mercedes-Benz Financial Services) → cart → Mercedes-Benz ID or guest → billing (private or business) → **choose the delivering retailer** → checkout with a **refundable holding deposit** → emailed confirmation with a reference. The Business Development Centre calls within 3 days to finalise variations, contract and payment. The contract is formed once you sign the Contract of Sale within 14 days.
- **Price:** the same standardised national price as at a retailer, **including on-road costs and pre-delivery**.

**(b) Standout:** true national stock visibility; one drive-away price everywhere; the customer chooses the retailer at checkout; online-exclusive launch allocations.

**(c) Numbers:** AU passenger car deliveries were 28,348 in 2021 and 19,989 in 2024, then 11,146 in the first half of 2025 (up 15.9%) (CarExpert via The Rural).

**(d) Weaknesses:** volumes fell after the switch to agency in January 2022. Dealer litigation (38 of 50 dealers, about $650m claimed) ended with Mercedes winning in 2023 and on appeal in July 2025. Online, you only reserve: a human call and paper contract follow.

**(e) Dealers:** **agency**. Mercedes owns the stock and sets the price, and retailers are paid agents for delivery. In the US it remains a franchise, with digital retailing through certified vendors (Motoinsight, Upstart, Drive Motors) (vendor releases, 2021–23; not opened).

## Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Account as order cockpit.** After the deposit, one login shows a task list (finance, trade-in, documents, payment, handover) and live status. Exhibited by Tesla, Rivian, Polestar, Mercedes AU (partly) and Ford (tracker only).
2. **Estimated delivery shown before and after purchase.** The configurator header (Tesla) or the order page shows a date that keeps updating (Polestar, Tesla). BMW AU and Ford add VIN-level and ship-level tracking.
3. **Configuration locks at VIN assignment.** Edits are allowed until a VIN is assigned, then frozen (Tesla, Ford, Polestar). Make this visible to the buyer.
4. **One price, published online, honoured in store.** Mercedes AU and Polestar AU (agency), Ford Model e, SmartPath's "research honoured in store", Amazon Autos all-in pricing, Genesis AU. Drive-away or on-road totals are standard in AU (Mercedes, Toyota dealer order page).
5. **Finance mode as a first-class switch.** Cash / lease / loan toggles change the page (Tesla AU Cash/Novated/Loan). Choosing finance limits options or extras (Polestar). Lenders are integrated (TFS, Hyundai Motor Finance, MBFS).
6. **Small online deposit; the dealer or OEM completes the deal.** Tesla's non-refundable order fee, Rivian's $100 refundable reservation, Toyota AU's $1,000 holding deposit, Mercedes' refundable holding deposit, Kia AU's $100. Most "buy online" really means reserve online, then a human call.
7. **A named human layered on top of self-serve.** Rivian Guide, Genesis Concierge or Experience Executive, Mercedes Business Development Centre, Polestar specialists, BMW Genius chat.
8. **Experiences that come to the customer.** Home test drives (Genesis, Hyundai), home delivery (Rivian, Polestar DE, Genesis where permitted), service valet (Genesis 90% use), virtual education sessions (Tesla).
9. **Trade-in folded into checkout** with a firm or third-party value (Amazon Autos, Tesla self-inspection, Hyundai KBB/Black Book/Edmunds). J.D. Power 2025: deal satisfaction is 800 when the dealer justifies the trade value and 672 when it doesn't.
10. **Return or exchange guarantees.** Hyundai 3-day exchange, Ford Blue Advantage 14-day return, Amazon used 3-day/300-mile return, Polestar AU 14-day cancellation. Tesla dropped its 7-day return.
11. **The pendulum swings back to dealers.** Polestar ("renaissance of the dealers"), Genesis AU (direct → agency), BMW (agency delayed to 2027), Hyundai AU Click to Buy apparently withdrawn. The winning model is OEM-set price and journey with dealer fulfilment: Toyota SmartPath, Mercedes agency, Ford Model e.
12. **OEM-built retail software integrated with dealer systems.** SmartPath's real-time DMS, CRM and website integration is the clearest precedent for a DMS vendor's configurator. Ford and Mercedes certify third-party digital retail vendors instead.
