# Track: Online used-car retailers and what they taught the industry

Researched 16 Oct 2026 for the Revolution Software / Toyota dealer configurator prototype. Every claim comes from a page listed under Sources. Where a live consumer site blocked automated access (carvana.com, carmax.com guarantee pages, motorpoint.co.uk, businesswire.com), I used the company's SEC filing, investor PDF or help centre instead, and say so. "Not found" means I did not find a credible published figure. It does not mean the figure is zero.

---

## Carvana (US)

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Discover / choose:** Mobile-first site with "advanced filtering and search" across a single national pool of 75,000+ units (31 Dec 2025). The 10-K says customer research shows "size and breadth of selection are primary determinants" of which retailer people choose. The AI "Shaqbot" (Sept 2025) uses Shaquille O'Neal's recorded voice, answers questions and browses inventory, with a "Shaq Filter". The 10-K also says AI chatbots support both customers and customer advocates.
- **Inspect:** A patented automated photo rig produces a 360-degree interior and exterior tour of the actual car, with **annotated high-definition imperfection call-outs**. It also builds a 3D model. All reconditioning sites work to one cosmetic standard.
- **Price:** Fixed and no-haggle. Carvana says it charges no dealer fees and called out the new FTC rules on fees in advertised prices (Q2 2026 letter).
- **Finance / trade-in:** A short pre-qualification form returns "nearly instantaneous" conditional terms on **every car in inventory**, using a soft pull. An interactive tool trades off deposit, monthly payment and term. The trade-in is "an automated, conditional offer" after a few questions, and can be used on a purchase or paid out as cash. A ConsumerAffairs reviewer reports the offer is valid for 7 days.
- **Buy:** Checkout is fully online, with the add-ons sold inside the flow: vehicle service contract, GAP, and Root insurance.
- **Deliver / return:** Home delivery by a Carvana employee in a branded hauler, **as soon as same day** in some markets (more than 20 states by Oct 2025). Customers can also collect from one of **39 car vending machines**. The 10-K says that after a vending machine opens, market penetration rises and variable cost per vehicle falls. There is a **seven-day return**: reporting cites 400 miles, and the 10-K does not state a cap. A limited warranty covers 100 days or 4,189 miles.
- **Own:** Customer advocates handle returns and exchanges. Most after-sales is limited to warranty, VSC and insurance.

**(b) Standouts:** imperfection-tagged 360-degree imagery; finance terms pre-computed for the whole inventory; vending machines as a brand theatre; logistics covering more than 80% of the US population, with 75% living within 100 miles of a reconditioning or auction site.

**(c) Numbers:** FY2025 retail units 596,641 (+43%), revenue $20.3B, adjusted EBITDA $2.24B (11.0%). Q2 2026: 197,325 units (+38%), revenue $7.38B, total GPU $7,014, adjusted EBITDA margin 10.4%. Its own surveys average 4.7/5 across 245,000+ responses. Trustpilot 4.0 from about 16,400 reviews, with 19% of them one-star. Market share is about 1.6% of US used retail. Target: 3M units a year.

**(d) Weaknesses:** Title and registration failures caused dealer-licence suspensions in North Carolina (2021), Illinois and Michigan (2022). In the Jan 2023 Illinois settlement, Carvana admitted to late titles and forfeited a $250k bond. Trustpilot complaints cover undisclosed defects found after delivery, trade-in offers that drop on resubmission, and slow refunds and registration paperwork. Truth in Advertising questioned whether the 7-day "money-back" return really is one once fees are counted.

**(e) Lesson:** Carvana survived because it owns the expensive middle of the chain (reconditioning capacity of about 1.5M a year, ADESA sites, logistics, and finance origination and sale). Its Q2 2026 letter says growth tracks **production capacity and delivery distance** by region, not marketing.

## CarMax (US)

**(a) Stage by stage**
- **Discover / choose:** More than 255 stores reaching 85% of the US population. The **Skye** assistant has run since 2020 and in FY25 answered more than half of customer questions on its own (+20% YoY). It now handles finance applications, vehicle transfers, appointments and appraisal offers. From Feb 2026 a **ChatGPT app** gives natural-language search over 45,000+ cars plus instant value estimates. From May 2026, 100% of inbound store and call-centre calls go first to **Sierra-built AI voice agents**, which hand off to associates.
- **Inspect:** Standard listing photos. Trustpilot reviewers complain that listings missed condition issues.
- **Price:** No-haggle upfront pricing, which reviewers praise because the out-the-door price does not change at signing.
- **Finance:** "Results in just 4 minutes with no impact to your credit". The site claims "Over 97% get pre-qualified" and multiple lenders. CarMax Auto Finance funded 42.8% of units in Q4 FY26.
- **Trade-in:** A real offer "in minutes" from licence plate or VIN, **valid 7 days**, paid on the spot after verification at a store or home pickup. "Offer Watch" tracks the car's value over time. CarMax bought 229,000 cars from consumers in Q4 FY26.
- **Buy:** Reserve online, finance and trade-in with remote support, then an online sales order. Customers can complete any mix of the four steps online.
- **Deliver / return:** Express pickup in store, or delivery within 60 miles of selected stores. Shipping fees between stores are paid upfront. The **Love Your Car Guarantee** launched in Jan 2021 with a 24-hour test drive and a 30-day / 1,500-mile return. **The return was cut to 10 days from 12 May 2024.** CarMax cited returns late in the window, people "working the system", and failing to finish DMV title paperwork after day 10. I did not verify whether the 24-hour test drive is still offered.
- **Own:** MaxCare service plans, service departments and a limited warranty (90 days / 4,000 miles as of 2021).

**(b) Standouts:** a published **omnichannel taxonomy** — "online" means all four steps done online, "omni" means some; AI deployed at scale in chat, phone and ChatGPT; plate-to-offer trade-in.

**(c) Numbers:** FY26 (to Feb 2026) retail used units 780,684 (−1.1%). In Q4 FY26 digital supported **83%** of retail sales, omni was **70%** and fully online **13%**. Q2 FY27: retail units 227,391 (+13.8%), revenue $7.9B, retail GPU $2,105, digital 81%. Trustpilot 4.5 from 3,851 reviews.

**(d) Weaknesses:** Shoppers report post-delivery defects, listings that missed damage, and registration delays. In FY26 CarMax lost volume and cut margin to win it back, booking a $141M goodwill impairment and changing CEO. The CEO says AI cannot close the deal because "each used car is an individual SKU" and buyers still want to see and drive the car.

**(e) Lesson:** A long return window is expensive because the paperwork clock (title and registration) runs out before the return clock does.

## Vroom (US): failed

**(a)** Fully online with no-haggle pricing, nationwide delivery (advertised as "typically 10 to 14 days"), a 7-day money-back guarantee, a 90-day bumper-to-bumper warranty, financing from 12+ banks and cash offers for sellers. It later bought the subprime lender UACC.
**(b)** National reach without stores; Texas Direct Auto for reconditioning.
**(c)** 2021: 74,698 e-commerce units, revenue $2.44B, **net loss $370.9M (about $4,970 of net loss for each e-commerce unit; the loss covers the whole company)**, Q4 2021 e-commerce GPU $1,548 with vehicle GPU only $473. E-commerce wound down on 22 Jan 2024 with 800 jobs cut. Chapter 11 in Nov 2024, emerged Jan 2025 as UACC plus CarStory.
**(d)** The FTC ($1M settlement, July 2024) alleged Vroom claimed thorough inspections that did not happen, missed its 10–14-day delivery promise (some waits over 3 months) without getting consent or refunding, and failed Used Car Rule Buyers Guide and warranty disclosures. 20,361 consumers were refunded in March 2025.
**(e)** Vroom showed that a **delivery-date promise and an inspection claim are legal commitments**. When they are made without your own reconditioning and logistics capacity, they turn into regulatory exposure.

## Shift (US): failed

**(a)** Shift began as consignment and moved to owning stock. Its signature was the **on-demand test drive** brought to the buyer's home or office. It offered a 150-point inspection, a 7-day / 200-mile return, financing, and DMV handling.
**(b)** Concierge test drive, which CEO George Arison called "our competitive advantage".
**(c)** 2021 revenue $637M. Q1 2021: 4,452 e-commerce units, net loss $42.8M (about $9,600 of net loss for each e-commerce unit; the loss covers the whole company). Merged with CarLotz in Dec 2022, cut 30% then 34% of staff in 2023, and announced a Chapter 11 wind-down on 6 Oct 2023 after failing to raise capital.
**(d)** No published satisfaction data found.
**(e)** A concierge test drive is a person and a car moved for every lead, before anything is sold. It scales linearly with cost.

## Cazoo (UK): collapsed

**(a)** Launched Dec 2019. Fixed prices, buy or finance fully online, delivery "in as little as 72 hours" or collection from a customer centre, 150+ point reconditioning, a **7-day / 250-mile money-back guarantee** with free collection, a 90-day warranty, RAC cover, and a "Car Care" add-on. Seller collection cost from £149.
**(b)** Speed (72 hours), detailed images, and its own reconditioning site.
**(c)** NYSE SPAC listing in 2021 at about US$7–8B. It lost £329M in 2021 and sold about 65,000 cars in 2022 against a 100,000 target. It left the EU in Sept 2022, closed 15 of 22 UK centres in 2023, stopped retailing in March 2024 and entered administration on 21 May 2024 owing about £259M unsecured. The brand was sold to Motors.co.uk.
**(d)** Administrator Teneo blamed "aggressive expansion strategies… high customer acquisition costs", plus marketing, sponsorship and "high-end logistics and reconditioning costs [that] outpaced revenue". In a Startline poll of 62 dealers, 50% blamed the limited potential of online-only selling, 37% said investors overestimated tech efficiencies, and 35% said investors underestimated incumbents' online skills.
**(e)** Free delivery, free returns and paid brand awareness each cost money on every car. A car that is expensive to buy in (chip-shortage prices) leaves no margin to absorb them.

## Cinch (UK)

**(a)** Online-first and owned by Constellation (BCA / We Buy Any Car). **14-day money-back guarantee** from the day after delivery, under 250 miles, with free collection in a 2-hour slot from 72 hours after the request and the £99 admin fee refunded. Credit broker for PCP and HP finance. Part-exchange. An app for servicing and MOT dates. New cars are now sold through sister company Marshall Motor Group.
**(c)** Year to March 2025: revenue above £1bn, pre-tax loss £100.3M, and it has never made a profit. Repeat customers are 10% of sales.
**(d / e)** Cinch has been moving away from pure online. It now runs **physical handover / test-drive / aftersales sites** (Northampton, Manchester, Birmingham and others) that cut delivery cost. It has quietly **started charging for home delivery** and dropped big sponsorships. These are the same cost lines that sank Cazoo.

## Motorpoint (UK): profitable omnichannel

**(a)** Nearly-new cars only, no-haggle pricing with a price promise, and QR-code prices on every car on display. Home delivery to mainland UK on weekdays from 10am to 5pm. **14-day money-back guarantee**, collected free within the first 7 days. "Sell Your Car" direct buying. Vehicle compare. Agentic AI follow-up on leads, natural-language AI chat, and an AI "digital discovery assistant".
**(b)** **NPS 84**, which it reports as a KPI. Agentic AI follow-up reactivated closed leads and was credited with **877 incremental sales** in FY26.
**(c)** FY26 (to Mar 2026): 64.6k retail units (+7.8%), revenue £1,268.6M, profit before tax £7.5M (+83%), 18.1M web sessions, Trustpilot 4.6 from 27k reviews, 21 stores with 30+ targeted. Sell Your Car bought 6,603 cars (+85%).
**(d)** Thin margin (PBT about 0.6% of revenue). Finance attachment is "below historic levels".
**(e)** Stores plus online stays profitable where online-only did not.

## Australia

### carsales (CAR Group): Instant Offer and Redbook
**(a)** **Instant Offer:** enter rego and state, and an offer from an accredited dealer appears "within a few minutes". It is **usually valid 7 days** and may show as a range if an inspection is needed. Cars carsales cannot price automatically get a manual offer in 1–2 business days. The dealer then inspects at the dealership or by mobile inspection, checking build year, odometer, condition, service history, write-off and title. Payment usually arrives **next business day** and the sale can finish "in as little as 24 hours". No roadworthy certificate is needed, and the seller is not bound until the contract is signed. **Redbook**, part of the carsales network, provides valuation reports, inspections and history checks. A third-party guide prices the report at about $33.
**(c)** H1 FY26: private revenue +5%, "reflects an increase in Instant Offer". Volumes are not published.
**(d)** The offer can be cut at inspection, which is a structural source of distrust.
**(e)** A marketplace can run the trade-in front end and leave the car to the dealer. This is the model closest to a franchised dealer's needs.

### Carma
**(a)** Sydney-based online retailer, founded 2021. Expert inspection and reconditioning (50+ in-house staff), "**7 days to drive and decide**", 3-month warranty, 12 months NRMA roadside, finance through partners, online trade-in valuation, and delivery (a Greater Sydney footnote applies).
**(c)** 5,000+ customers by March 2025, A$30M convertible note (Mar 2025) after a $28M seed and $75M Series A. Google 4.6 from 1.2k reviews, ProductReview 4.7 from 537.
**(e)** Venture-funded and still small, so it is open to the same Cazoo and Vroom unit-economics test.

### Cars24 Australia
**(a)** Still operating. It has not exited Australia. Hubs in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane. "**30-Day Return Policy**", 300-point inspection, roadworthy certificate and PPSR check included, a car condition report, "12x faster" Fastrack Finance from 30+ lenders, and a "100% online" process.
**(c)** Claims 30,000+ customers. Trustpilot 4.3 from 712 reviews (the company does not invite reviews).
**(d)** Offers changed after the quote, delivery delays, AI-sounding support replies, and one report of a car failing a roadworthy after passing its inspection. The parent quit Indonesia and Saudi Arabia in 2023 to focus on core markets, Australia included.

### easyauto123 (Eagers Automotive)
**(a)** Used-car superstores under a franchised-dealer group, explicitly "modelled on CarMax" (GoAuto, 2020). Haggle-free fixed prices, a **7-day money-back guarantee**, inspection and history check, 12 months 24/7 roadside, finance pre-approval, and appraisal-based sell or trade. 500+ cars at a site.
**(b)** Stock comes from trade-ins at Eagers' roughly 200 franchised dealerships. The initial goal was a 1:1 new-to-used ratio, about 100,000 used cars a year.
**(e)** **This is the most direct template for a Toyota dealer group**: the franchised network feeds the CarMax-style used retail engine.

### Pickles
**(a)** An auction-led remarketer: PicklesLIVE and PicklesONLINE auctions, government, fleet, salvage and prestige sales, and "Cars to Buy Now" at fixed prices. EV listings show an independent **battery-health certificate via QR code**. Finance, transport, warranty and Sell Your Car (self-appraisal).
**(e)** Shows buyers will accept less hand-holding in exchange for a wholesale price. The EV battery certificate is a condition-transparency idea that transfers well.

## Amazon Autos (US): newest entrant

**(a)** Launched Dec 2024 with Hyundai in 48 cities. Shoppers browse local dealer stock by model, trim, colour and features, get an **instant trade-in valuation**, finance or pay in full, check out online, then **schedule a pickup at the dealer**. The price at checkout is final, with taxes and fees included. Used and CPO were added in 2025, starting in Los Angeles, with Chase, Santander and Wells Fargo as lenders.
**(b)** **Dealers stay the seller of record**: they set the price, pay to list and finish the paperwork. Amazon is the storefront.
**(c)** By 2026 it covers six brands named in April 2026 reporting (Hyundai, Kia, Mazda, Subaru, Chevrolet, Jeep), though one source counts seven, and Ford's CPO program joined in Nov 2025 in 130+ cities. Hyundai says it covers 80% of the US market by dealer enrolment, and **78% of buyers are new to Hyundai**. It plans to go international from 2027 and has confirmed the UK.
**(d)** Dealers report the experience "beginning to degrade" as traffic grows, with incomplete paperwork and inventory mismatches. One South Bay Hyundai dealer saw Amazon sales fall from about 10 a month to about 5.
**(e)** This is the agency-style blueprint for franchised new cars. It also shows that **real-time dealer inventory and document accuracy** are the hard part.

## Cross-cutting patterns

1. **No-haggle, all-in pricing is table stakes.** Seen at Carvana, CarMax, Motorpoint, easyauto123, Cazoo and Amazon (tax and fees included at checkout). The FTC fee rules are now forcing it. *Transfers directly:* show a drive-away price in the configurator.
2. **Instant, conditional trade-in offers with a validity window.** CarMax (minutes, 7 days), carsales Instant Offer (minutes, 7 days, paid next business day), Carvana, Amazon. The universal friction is **post-inspection re-pricing**. *Transfers:* integrate Instant Offer or Redbook and make the conditions explicit up front.
3. **Soft-pull pre-qualification priced against real stock.** Carvana (terms for every car), CarMax (4 minutes, 97% qualify), Cars24 (30+ lenders). *Transfers:* show a personalised repayment on each configured build, not a generic calculator.
4. **Condition transparency beats glossy photos.** Carvana's imperfection-tagged 360-degree tours, carsales' inspection checklist, Pickles' EV battery certificate. Defects missed in listings top the complaints at both Carvana and CarMax. *Partial transfer:* for new cars, show build or ETA status and the exact stock vehicle. For trade-ins and demos, show the condition.
5. **Return windows are trust signals with real costs.** 7 days (Carvana, Carma, easyauto123, Cazoo), 10 days (CarMax, cut from 30), 14 days (Cinch, Motorpoint), 30 days (Cars24). The window must close before title and registration paperwork does. *Limited transfer to new cars;* a test-drive-at-home or "change-your-mind before delivery" promise is more realistic.
6. **Paperwork (title, rego, Buyers Guide) is where online retailers get punished.** Carvana's licence suspensions, Vroom's FTC case, Amazon dealers' incomplete paperwork. *Transfers:* the DMS has to own the e-contract, rego and compliance data. This is the vendor's core advantage.
7. **Omnichannel wins over pure online.** CarMax is 70% omni versus 13% fully online. Motorpoint is profitable with stores. Cinch is adding sites. Cazoo and Vroom died online-only. *Transfers strongly:* the dealer is the fulfilment point, so the configurator should hand off cleanly by letting the customer complete any subset of steps.
8. **Delivery speed comes from owned logistics and nearby stock, not promises.** Carvana ties regional growth to production capacity. Vroom's 10–14-day promise became an FTC case. *Transfers:* show honest ETAs from live stock or allocation, never optimistic defaults.
9. **AI is moving from chat to agentic operations.** Skye (more than 50% resolved), Sierra voice on 100% of CarMax calls, CarMax's ChatGPT app, Motorpoint's agentic follow-up (877 sales), Carvana's Shaqbot. *Transfers:* AI lead follow-up and guided choice help, with a human hand-off at deal stage.
10. **Free delivery, free returns and paid brand awareness are the costs that kill.** Cazoo (sponsorship, logistics), Cinch (now charges for delivery), Shift (concierge drives). *Lesson for the prototype:* make convenience options visible and priced, not unconditionally free.
11. **Captive finance carries the economics.** Carvana sells its loans, CarMax Auto Finance funded 42.8% of units, Vroom bought UACC too late. *Transfers:* finance and F&I products embedded in checkout (Carvana sells VSC, GAP and insurance inline).
12. **Dealer-as-fulfilment marketplaces are the franchised future.** Amazon Autos, carsales Instant Offer and easyauto123 all keep the franchised dealer as seller and handover point. *Transfers directly* to a Toyota dealer configurator.
