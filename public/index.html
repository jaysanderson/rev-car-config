Assumptions in this mockup

Fictional dealer and marqueHarbourline, Kestrel and Ridge are invented. Prices, stock, ages and bonuses are illustrative and chosen to show each behaviour at least once.
Four questions are wired upThe suggested chips return scripted answers with the trace the real agent would produce. Free-text questions get a placeholder reply.
Domo is read through its API, not embeddedThe page never shows a Domo card. The experience consumes published datasets and renders them in its own language.
Stock refresh cadenceAssumed 15 minutes for stock and nightly for ageing and margin. Fast enough to never show a sold car for long, slow enough to keep DMS load trivial.
Discounts are explainedAged stock is offered with the reason stated. The customer should never feel steered without knowing why.
Assistant language onlySwitching language translates the assistant here. In production the whole page follows the customer.
